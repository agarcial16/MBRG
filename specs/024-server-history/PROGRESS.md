## M2 snapshot race fixed (settled bound; user said "fix it now")
- The race (found by the Opus review): the finish instant is `last_step_ms`, and a step run on time keeps its SCHEDULED instant, up to `TOLERANCE_MS` (1,000 ms) before the instant it is committed at. A match finishing just after the first page of a walk could carry an instant older than the head, sort inside the snapshot, change its total and (when behind the cursor) be skipped.
- Fix (read side only, no schema or cursor change): a match is listed only once it is SETTLED, i.e. `last_step_ms < now - TOLERANCE_MS` on the server clock. Any match that finishes after a read has an instant >= that read's `now - TOLERANCE_MS`, so it always sorts newer than the head the read took and the head bound excludes it. Cost: a finished match shows in the history about 1 s after its finish (FR-561 text amended).
