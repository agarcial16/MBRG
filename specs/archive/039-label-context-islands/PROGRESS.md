## 2026-11-26 — Implementation, visual verification and archive
- **Subordinate label context** (`apps/web/src/core/label-context.ts`, `label-frame.ts`, `label-readable.ts`, `ui/map-view.ts`): subordinate territory labels (`territoryName !== factionName`) render at 45 % opacity at province zoom and ramp smoothly to 100 % between `3.0 * detail` and `4.5 * detail` (viewer + capture, FR-727). In round-capture mode (`frame=round`, `k >= 1`), non-absorber labels attenuate to 35 %, the absorber's faction label stays pinned at 100 % above `fadeStart`, and its homeland territory label is suppressed when pinned so the absorber name is never duplicated (FR-728). Step transitions keep subordinate opacity smooth (FR-734).
- **Pacific-style tiny island markers** (`apps/web/src/core/island-markers.ts`, `render/labels-layer.ts`, `ui/map-view.ts`): pure island territories (`nbStart[p] === nbStart[p + 1]`) with `mainExtent <= 1.2 %` of the map's longer side are grouped into spatial clusters once at load (NFR-193), drawn as rounded dashed maritime borders in the owning faction's colour with dark halo and faint tint, full strength `<= 10 px`, fading `10..18 px`, responsive minimum box size (`10..16 px`) on mobile and desktop, dimmed consistently in tours and captures (FR-729..FR-731).
- **Main-piece capture framing** (`apps/web/src/core/capture.ts`, `ui/capture-main.ts`): `mainAnnexationBox` excludes tiny distant outlier pieces (`< 2 %` total area and `> 1.5x` main span) while keeping the main landmass, significant conquered pieces, and island-only fallback (FR-732, FR-733).
