## Fix: floating cards on the phone (user report, 2026-11-10)
The user saw the round banner and the state card ("Ganadora…", not live) land under the minimap and not be visible when testing the phone layout in a browser.
- Reproduced a real fragility: the state card sits at a fixed `top: 168px` (assumes a ~102 px minimap), but the minimap canvas was `width: 100%; height: auto`, so a tall map made it ~194 px high and it overlapped the card (`fix-phone-tallmap-before.png`). On short screens the dock (about 300 px) could also cover a card or the centred banner.
- Fix (CSS, `live-panel-style.ts`): phone minimap canvas capped at 92 px (aspect kept); `--hud-pane-h` shared by the dock pane, the card `max-height` (stops above the dock) and the banner `padding-bottom` (centres in the map area above the dock).
- Tests: 4 red then green in `test/play-shell.test.ts`. `npm test` 328 files / 5199 passed; typecheck green.
- Browser: 390x844 with a simulated tall minimap and 360x640 (`fix-phone-*-after.png`). Not reproduced with a real portrait map (only the dev map exists): the tall minimap was simulated by resizing the canvas.
