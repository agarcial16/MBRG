# Spec 010 — Interface revamp (the match screen)
Status: approved (by the user on 2026-10-27, Light SDD). Tier: Light SDD (UI, visual design). Depends on 008 and 009 (implemented). Ids continue 009: FR-356 to FR-368, NFR-65 to NFR-71.

## Goal
One coherent visual system for the live match screen, on desktop and phone: the map fills the screen and a historic-RTS style HUD frames it at the edges (no floating sheet). The spec fixes the layout, the visual tokens, the states and basic accessibility. It adds the places where the player's rank, points and prediction will appear, but their data and behaviour belong to spec 011 (play in the browser). The bot is spec 012.

The reference is the clickable mockup `specs/010-interface-revamp/mockup/hist.html` (open it through a local web server; the map photo and the parchment image sit beside it). The user accepted it as the first concept on 2026-10-27. Where the mockup and this text differ, this text wins.

## User decisions (chat, 2026-10-27)
- Direction: RTS-like HUD with an old, historic touch (campaign diary, wax seal for "already predicted", parchment). Rounds as plain numbers, not Roman numerals.
- Minimap bottom right; chronicle and factions on the left; leaderboard on the right.
- Outside the map: "terra incognita" parchment. The image (AI-generated by the user) may be cropped and used.
- The previous/next controls must show clearly when the view is not live.
- Must scale to maps with tens or hundreds of factions and a faction with many neighbours.
- The factions list shows when a faction fell, and how many factions it eliminated and how much territory it conquered; sortable.

## Requirements
**Layout**
- FR-356: At 1024 px wide or more the map fills the viewport and the HUD occupies five zones: top bar, left column (chronicle above factions), right column (leaderboard), bottom right (minimap), bottom centre (command panel).
- FR-357: Under 1024 px: a compact top bar (round n/N, countdown, player chip) and a bottom dock with three tabs (Partida, Orden, Ranking) that opens upwards; the minimap is a small corner card; with the dock folded the map keeps at least 60 % of the height.
- FR-358: The top bar shows round n of N, the phase, the factions alive, the countdown and a player chip. The chip is an empty slot in this spec (hidden until spec 011 fills it).
- FR-359: The minimap shows the whole map with the current view rectangle and moves the camera on tap or drag. It replaces the "reset view" control (kept as a button).

**Information**
- FR-360: The chronicle lists one entry per applied round, newest first: round number, victim, absorber and territories taken. The round still pending shows only what the reveal made public (anti-spoiler, NFR-71). A "who guessed right" line is rendered only when result data exists; in this spec it stays hidden (spec 011).
- FR-361: The factions list has columns Terr., Bajas and Conq., and sorts by any of them. Bajas = rounds in which the faction was the absorber; Conq. = territories it absorbed in total. A fallen faction is struck through and says "cayó en la ronda n". Search matches a faction name or a round. With more than 12 factions the list shows the first 12 for the chosen order, the count ("187 vivas de 240") and a "ver todas" paged view.
- FR-362: Bajas, Conq. and the fall round are computed in the browser from the public records of rounds 1 to N, with no server change. A late joiner reads the missing records on demand, in order, at the existing request limits, and the columns show a dash until they are known (open question OQ1).

**Live and replay**
- FR-363: When the displayed round is the live round the HUD shows an "en directo" mark and "Siguiente" is disabled. When it is an earlier round the HUD shows a band "Repasando la ronda k, el directo va por la ronda N" with a "Volver al directo" button, the round track marks both positions, and predicting is not offered.
- FR-364: The round track shows every round as a dot up to 12 rounds and a slider with a live marker above that.

**States and entry**
- FR-365: Loading, reconnecting, error (unknown match, digest mismatch, unsupported browser, insecure origin) and finished (winner, with the option to review any round) are separate states of the HUD. Each has a title, one plain sentence saying what happened and, when useful, one action. All texts live in `core/strings.ts`, in Spanish, sentence case.
- FR-366: One entry page. Without a match in the address it shows a start screen (match code input and its link form); with one it shows the match screen. The debug scene and the web test client remain development tools: reachable only in development builds, absent from the production bundle.

**Visual system**
- FR-367: Tokens (palette: wood, gilt, vellum, ink, wax, verdigris; type scale; spacing; frame). Type: Cormorant SC for titles and figures, Spectral for text, with lining tabular figures. Fonts are self-hosted files under `apps/web/public/fonts` with their licences (no CDN, no new npm dependency).
- FR-368: Terra incognita: where the map does not cover the screen, a parchment texture (one cropped tile of the user's image, repeated without visible seams, at most 150 KB) is drawn behind the map and moves with it. The faction colours stay the only saturated colours on screen.

**Accessibility and quality**
- NFR-65: Every text and control pair of the tokens has a contrast ratio of 4.5:1 (3:1 for text of 24 px or more and for control outlines), checked by a test over the token table.
- NFR-66: Touch targets are at least 44 x 44 CSS px in the phone layout; every control works with the keyboard and shows a visible focus; the countdown is not announced every second (state changes are, politely).
- NFR-67: With reduced motion the HUD has no animation beyond instant changes of state (the tours already follow the 009 rules).
- NFR-68: The HUD adds no per-frame layout work to the map and refreshes at most once per second apart from events; the 009 frame budgets still hold.
- NFR-69: Measured on a real phone (recorded in the PR): WebGL map with the 007 labels, first screen, scroll and tour frame times. A budget that cannot be met is reported, not hidden.
- NFR-70: Every visible text is in the strings module, in Spanish. No i18n.
- NFR-71: No regression: the anti-spoiler rule (no future round, no absorber before its reveal), the 008 and 009 scans and goldens, and the map format are unchanged.

## Out of scope
Creating or joining matches, lobby, player identity, entering predictions, leaderboard data and "who guessed right" data (spec 011); the Discord bot (012); i18n; editor, importers, 3D; changes to the server, the protocol, the sim or the map format.

## Open questions
- OQ1: Bajas/Conq. for a late joiner need the records of every earlier round. With hundreds of rounds that is hundreds of reads; the alternative is a server aggregate (a protocol change, spec 011 or later). Proposal: read on demand, newest to oldest, and accept a dash meanwhile.
- OQ2: The order of the candidate neighbours (for example by shared border length) is an input of spec 011 and needs data the server does not send today.

## Done criteria
1. Tests for each id above (pure parts in Node); typecheck and CI green; `git diff` shows no change in `packages/*`, `apps/server/src`, `tools`, `maps`.
2. Reviewed screenshots of the live screen from the local live fixture, at 1280 x 720 and 390 x 844, for: live, replay, loading, reconnecting, error, finished; the user accepts them.
3. The phone measurement of NFR-69 recorded.
4. The branch is one PR into `rewrite`; if it passes about 3,000 changed lines it splits into: (a) tokens, fonts, layout and states; (b) chronicle, factions, replay track and minimap; (c) terra incognita and the entry page.
