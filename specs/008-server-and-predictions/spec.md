# Spec 008 — Server and predictions
Status: approved (by the user on 2026-10-21, in block: D1 to D24 and P31 to P45, recorded as D25; amended by D26, user-approved 2026-10-22, by D27, user-approved 2026-10-23, by D28, user-approved 2026-10-23, and by D29, user-approved 2026-10-23)

## Context and goal
Roadmap step 3 is "server + predictions". Steps 1 and 2 are done (specs 001 to 007, merged into `rewrite`): a pure deterministic engine gives the match state and round records of any round from (map, seed, round) (001 FR-43, FR-44), and a browser viewer draws a state, with a debug scene that computes rounds locally from a seed typed into the page address (004 FR-156 to FR-161, 005 FR-187). Nothing runs a match for several people yet, and nobody can predict anything.

This spec defines:
1. **Live matches** run by a server: created on a map with host-configured timing, advanced by a **server clock** and by a **manual advance**, persisted so that a restart resumes at the right point.
2. **Two-phase rounds** (D8): in phase 1 players predict which faction is eliminated; at the phase-1 cutoff the victim is revealed; in phase 2 players predict which neighbour absorbs it; after the phase-2 cutoff and a locked wait the round is applied. Players also predict the **winner** of the match. Players are identified only by a **namespaced opaque player id** (D10). Predictions are scored against the server's own simulation, with inverse-probability points (D12).
3. **Results** per phase, per round and per match, per player id, readable by any client, so that the Discord bot of step 4 can say who guessed right and show a leaderboard.
4. The **anti-spoiler rule** for a server: a client receives only what has been revealed (the state, past rounds and, during phase 2, the victim), and cannot derive anything unrevealed from what it receives.
5. How the existing viewer **watches a live match**, while the debug scene stays a local developer tool.

Why: the game is a spectator battle royale; predicting is the only way a player takes part, so a match must run on its own (clock), be the same for everybody (one server, one engine), keep its future secret (otherwise predictions are pointless) and survive restarts (a match may last days). Two phases give each round two moments of suspense and make the absorber prediction a guess about a known victim.

### Why a 32-bit seed cannot be the secret of a live match
The engine draws round k from `roundRng(seed, k)` with a 32-bit seed (001 FR-31). The map and the engine code are public (the engine also runs in the browser), and every resolved round record is public. Anyone can therefore try all 2^32 (about 4.3 × 10^9) seeds offline and keep those that reproduce the published rounds: one candidate costs a few dozen integer operations for round 1, so the whole search takes minutes on a PC. Each round publishes about log2(living factions × absorber candidates) bits; on a map with about 70 factions that is about 8 bits per round, so after roughly 4 to 6 rounds a single seed is left and every later round is known. On the 5-faction dev map a whole match publishes fewer than 32 bits, so the leak does not show there, but it would on the target maps. Decision D5 therefore derives each round's seed from a 256-bit secret with a keyed function, so that past rounds say nothing about future ones.

### What is derived when (one seed per round, two reveals)
The engine's round step draws the victim and then the absorber from one stream, `roundRng(s_k, k)` (001, 002): both come from the same round seed s_k. The server computes s_k and runs the round step at the phase-1 close of round k, because it needs the victim then; the same computation also yields the absorber. The absorber and the state of round k are therefore **known to the server during phase 2 but kept secret**: never sent, stored or logged before the round is applied (FR-256), and recomputed from s_k at the application (P26). Under the threat model (D14) this non-storage is defence in depth only. Revealing the victim discloses about log2(living factions) bits of the round-k stream; anyone may enumerate the 2^32 values of s_k consistent with that victim, but their absorbers are spread over the candidates as evenly as the engine's draw (001 fairness), so the victim gives no usable advantage about the absorber (verified, NFR-47).

### Threat model (D14)
- **Trusted**: the operator, the server host, and anyone who can read the database file or its backups. Match secrets, the host-token memo of FR-238 and every other stored value are kept **in plain form** in the database; whoever reads it can compute every future round of every running match. Encryption at rest is out of scope (candidate for a later spec).
- **Untrusted**: every client (viewer, web test client, bot, any HTTP caller), with full knowledge of the engine, the map and all public data. The anti-spoiler requirements protect against them.
- **Transport**: confidentiality on the network (TLS) belongs to deployment, out of scope; without it a network observer can read credentials, but still no unrevealed outcome (the server never sends one).
- Consequence: FR-256 (absorber never stored before the application) is defence in depth, not a security boundary.

## Users
- **Player**: predicts through a client (the web test client now, the Discord bot in step 4); never controls the match.
- **Spectator**: watches a live match in the viewer.
- **Host**: configures and creates a match (with the operator credential) and may advance it by hand with the host token returned at creation.
- **Operator**: runs the server; holds the operator credential, the client credentials and the server settings (map list, limits, allowed origins) in the server's environment.
- **Client integrator**: writes a client (web test client, Discord bot) against the server interface, with a client credential.
- **Developer / reviewer (the user)**: runs tests with a fake clock and fixed secrets; keeps using the debug scene.

## User stories
- US-22. As a host, I want to create a match on a map with my own interval and phase leads, so that it plays by itself at the pace I choose.
- US-23. As a host or tester, I want to advance a match by hand, so that I can run a match quickly or recover a stuck one.
- US-24. As a player, I want to predict the victim, then the absorber once the victim is known, and the winner, each until a clear cutoff, so that I take part without controlling anything.
- US-25. As a player, I want to resubmit or retry a prediction safely, so that a network retry never doubles or loses it.
- US-26. As a player, I want to be sure nobody outside the operator can know anything not yet revealed, so that predictions are fair.
- US-27. As a spectator, I want the viewer to show a live match, the revealed victim and each applied round, so that I can watch without spoilers.
- US-28. As the Discord bot (step 4), I want per-phase and final results per player id and a leaderboard, with live events, so that I can announce who guessed right.
- US-29. As a host, I want a match to resume at the right point after a server restart or a delay, with full phases for players, so that a long match survives maintenance.
- US-30. As a developer, I want the server's match results to be the engine's results for fixed secrets, under a fake clock, so that tests are deterministic and golden.

## Definitions
- **Live match** (match): a match run by the server, with a **match id**, a map, a **match secret**, a timing setting, a schedule, predictions and results. Distinct from the debug scene's local match.
- **Map list**: a server setting mapping each **map id** (1 to 64 characters of `a-z`, `0-9`, `-`, for example `dev`) to a folder holding a map package (`map.json`, `ids.png`, `presentation.json`). This is how the operator makes a map available; the server reads and validates each listed package at start and before every step (FR-250).
- **Map digest**: SHA-256, in lowercase hexadecimal, of the canonical byte form of a map package: for each of `map.json`, `ids.png`, `presentation.json`, in this order, the UTF-8 file name, one zero byte, the file length as an 8-byte unsigned big-endian number, then the file's bytes exactly as stored (no re-encoding).
- **State digest**: SHA-256, in lowercase hexadecimal, of the ASCII text `classic;<round>;<t1>:<f1>,<t2>:<f2>,...` built from a `MatchState` (decimal integers without leading zeros or signs, owners in map order, no spaces).
- **Match secret**: 256 random bits held only by the server (D5). **Round seed** s_k: the 32-bit seed the engine uses for round k, derived from the match secret and k (FR-252).
- **Commitment**: the SHA-256 digest of the match secret, published at creation (D5).
- **Applied round N**: the number of rounds whose result has been applied to the published state (0 at creation). **Pending round** k = N + 1, while N < T.
- **Step**: one of exactly three operations on the pending round: **phase-1 close** (victim predictions end, the victim is revealed), **phase-2 close** (absorber predictions end), **application** (the absorber, record and state of the round become public and the next round starts). Each step is stored as one step row (FR-293). One advance or one late catch-up performs exactly one step (D22).
- **Phase 1** of round k: victim predictions are open, until the phase-1 close at C1. **Phase 2**: the victim is public; absorber predictions are open, until the phase-2 close at C2. **Locked**: between the phase-2 close and the application (lasts L2; empty when L2 = 0). **Application** at A.
- **Stage** of a match: `phase-1`, `phase-2`, `locked` (of the pending round), `finished` or `halted`. A match is **running** in the first three stages.
- **Instant**: a whole number of milliseconds since 1970-01-01T00:00:00Z (UTC). Every stored, scheduled, sent and compared time of this spec is an instant; timing values (I, L1, L2) are whole seconds, converted × 1,000.
- **Timing setting** (D11): either `manual` (no clock) or the three host values **interval** I (seconds from one round's start to its application, i.e. the whole round), **phase-1 lead** L1 (seconds from C1 to C2, the length of phase 2) and **phase-2 lead** L2 (seconds from C2 to A). For a round **anchored** at instant t0: C1 = t0 + I − L1 − L2, C2 = C1 + L1, A = C2 + L2 = t0 + I.
- **Schedule anchors** of a running match: the stored t0, C1, C2 and A of the pending round as currently scheduled, after any shift (FR-244, FR-248). Anchors are computed instants, usually in the future; storing them does not change the last step instant.
- **Last step instant** of a match: the instant of its latest stored step row (the creation instant before any step). It is updated **only** by storing a step row; predictions, rejections, reads, replies and anchor changes never update it.
- **Effective instant** of a match: max(server clock reading, last step instant) (FR-249). Every cutoff decision, every prediction's stored instant and every step instant uses it.
- **Reported time**: the time the server sends in replies and events of a match: max(effective instant, the largest reported time already sent for that match by this process). It never decreases within a process and is never below any instant already sent for the match (reveal and application instants included), so ages computed from it (reported time − an instant of the match) are never negative.
- **On time / overdue**: a step is on time when it is performed at most 1,000 ms (the NFR-50 tolerance) after its scheduled instant, and overdue when later.
- **Cutoff** of a prediction: C1 for a victim prediction, C2 for an absorber prediction, the phase-1 close of round T for a winner prediction. Exclusive: a prediction counts only if stored at an effective instant strictly before it and before the step that closes it began.
- **Closed** phase: its closing step (phase-1 close, or phase-2 close) has been stored. **Scored** phase: its results have been stored (the phase-1 close for victim predictions, the application for absorber predictions, the end of the match for winner predictions). A phase can be closed and not yet scored (phase 2 while `locked`).
- **Prediction kinds** (D2, D8): **victim** (round k, phase 1), **absorber** (round k, phase 2), **winner** (match).
- **Winner candidates** at a moment: the factions living at N, minus the victim of the pending round once revealed.
- **Credential**: a secret presented by a caller: the **operator credential**, a **client credential** (each with a **namespace**, for example `web`, `discord`) or a match's **host token**. Credential texts use only `A-Z`, `a-z`, `0-9`, `-`, `_` (base64url or hexadecimal), 22 to 256 characters.
- **Local player id**: the id a client sends for one of its players. **Player id** (namespaced): `<namespace>:<local id>`, built by the server from the client credential's namespace (FR-284).
- **Client request id**: an optional id chosen by the caller to make a command idempotent (FR-282). **Same content** of two commands: the same method, path and canonical body, where the canonical body is the JSON body without the request-id field, with object keys sorted and no insignificant whitespace.
- **Network address**: the IP address of the TCP peer of the connection; forwarding headers are not trusted (a proxy setup is out of scope).
- **Server clock**: the only time source of the server layer; injectable (FR-314).
- **Public data** of a match: match id, map id and map digest, T, timing setting, commitment, stage, schedule anchors C1, C2, A of the pending round when the clock runs, reported time, N, state at N and its state digest, the **last event sequence number** (FR-289(b)), the victim fields of FR-288 during `phase-2` and `locked` (and in `halted` when the victim of the halting round was revealed), the winner and match secret once finished, the halt reason once halted.
- **Event**: a message the server pushes to subscribers of a match, with a per-match **sequence number** starting at 1 (FR-289).
- **Server settings**: operator values read from the environment at start: map list, credentials, rate limits (FR-299), allowed origins (FR-286), the running-match limit (default 20, D23).

## Functional requirements

### Match creation and timing (D10, D11)
- FR-231: WHEN a caller presents the operator credential and asks to create a match with a map id of the map list, a timing setting and a client request id, THE SYSTEM SHALL create the match at round 0 (the state `getState(map, 0, 0)`), stage `phase-1` of round 1, draw a new match secret, match id and host token, store them with the first event (FR-289(c)) before replying, and reply with the match id, the public data at round 0 and the host token.
- FR-232: IF the creation request is invalid, THEN THE SYSTEM SHALL create nothing and reply with the first applicable error of FR-301: `forbidden` (no operator credential), `unknown-map` (a map id not in the map list), the map's 001 validation errors, `too-few-factions` (FR-237), `invalid-timing` (FR-233), or `too-many-matches` when the running-match limit is reached.
- FR-233: THE SYSTEM SHALL accept as timing setting either `manual` or values I, L1 and L2 set by the host, and SHALL reject with `invalid-timing`, naming each failed rule, only impossible or absurd values (D11): (a) I or L1 not a whole number ≥ 1; (b) L2 not a whole number ≥ 0 (P23); (c) I ≤ L1 + L2 (phase 1 would be empty); (d) I above 2,592,000 (30 days, P24). Every other combination SHALL be accepted as the host chose it. A JSON number is required for each value; a missing timing field, a missing value or a value that is not a JSON number is malformed and rejected with `invalid-request` (FR-296), checked before these rules (R6).
- FR-234: THE SYSTEM SHALL NOT fill in a missing timing value; any default preset is a client matter (P25).
- FR-235: THE SYSTEM SHALL keep a match's map, map digest and timing setting fixed after creation (D11).
- FR-236: THE SYSTEM SHALL draw the match id from a cryptographic random source, independently of the match secret, with at least 96 random bits, and the host token with 256 random bits, both in the credential character set.
- FR-237: IF the map has fewer than 2 factions (T = 0), THEN THE SYSTEM SHALL reject the creation with `too-few-factions`; WHEN T = 1, THE SYSTEM SHALL run the single round normally, the winner predictions closing at its phase-1 close (FR-266).
- FR-238: THE SYSTEM SHALL require a client request id on every creation request, and for a repeated creation with the same operator credential, request id and same content within the retention of FR-282 SHALL return the first reply again, host token included, creating no second match (P36, D23); the memo of that reply is the only stored copy of a host token in plain form and SHALL be deleted when the 24-hour retention ends, the token's one-way digest being kept.

### Round timeline (D8, D16, D22)
- FR-239: WHILE the clock runs (timing not `manual`) and the match is running, THE SYSTEM SHALL perform each step of the pending round when the effective instant reaches its scheduled instant from the schedule anchors: phase-1 close at C1, phase-2 close at C2, application at A; round 1 is anchored at t0 = the creation instant, every later round at the instant of the previous application.
- FR-240: THE SYSTEM SHALL store each step as one step row (round, kind, instant, cause: `clock`, `manual` or `late`); WHEN the application falls due at the same instant as the phase-2 close (L2 = 0), THE SYSTEM SHALL store both rows, phase-2 close first, in one storage transaction with the same instant, and publish their events in that order.
- FR-241: WHEN phase 1 of round k closes, THE SYSTEM SHALL, in one storage transaction: stop accepting victim predictions for round k, compute s_k and the engine's round step from the stored state at k − 1, store the step row, the victim with its reveal instant (the step instant) and the phase-1 results (FR-271); then publish the FR-288 fields and the stage `phase-2`.
- FR-242: WHEN phase 2 of round k closes, THE SYSTEM SHALL stop accepting absorber predictions for round k, store the step row, and publish the stage `locked` (unless the application is stored in the same transaction, FR-240).
- FR-243: WHEN round k is applied, THE SYSTEM SHALL recompute the round step from the stored state at k − 1 and s_k, and IF its victim differs from the stored victim THEN halt the match with reason `victim-mismatch` (FR-251); otherwise store, in one transaction, the step row, the state and record of round k, the phase-2 results (FR-272) and, for k < T, the anchors of round k + 1; then publish them (or finish the match after round T, FR-246).
- FR-244: WHEN a caller presents the operator credential or the match's host token and asks to advance a running match, naming the step it expects (round and kind), THE SYSTEM SHALL decide it by FR-295 and, if the named step is the next step, perform that one step at once, at the effective instant t, with cause `manual` (D22): in `phase-1` the phase-1 close; in `phase-2` the phase-2 close only; in `locked` the application. WHILE the clock runs, THE SYSTEM SHALL then store shifted anchors computed from t as if it were the instant of the step performed: (a) after a phase-1 close at t, C2 = t + L1 and A = t + L1 + L2; (b) after a phase-2 close at t, A = t + L2 (with L2 = 0 the application falls due at t and FR-240 applies); (c) after an application at t, round k + 1 is anchored at t0 = t. With timing `manual`, each advance performs one step, so a round takes three advances.
- FR-245: IF an advance is requested without the operator credential or the match's host token, THEN THE SYSTEM SHALL reply `forbidden`; on a finished match `match-finished`; on a halted match `match-halted`; nothing changes.
- FR-246: WHEN round T is applied, THE SYSTEM SHALL mark the match finished, record the winner (the only living faction), stop its clock, score the winner predictions (FR-273), and publish the final leaderboard and the match secret (FR-259); no `schedule` event follows (FR-287).
- FR-247: THE SYSTEM SHALL perform each step of a match exactly once and in order, whatever the triggers (clock, advances, late catch-up); which trigger performs a step and what the others get is decided by FR-295.
- FR-248: WHEN a scheduled step is overdue, for any cause (server start after downtime, a storage failure and its retry, a stalled process, a forward jump of the clock), THE SYSTEM SHALL: (a) perform only that one step, as a manual advance at the effective instant (FR-244, cause `late`), counting only predictions stored before its cutoff; (b) store shifted anchors from that instant by FR-244(a) to (c), so that players always get a full phase and the locked wait is kept (D16, D22); (c) never perform several overdue steps in a row. An on-time step SHALL be stored with its scheduled instant and SHALL NOT shift the anchors.
- FR-249: THE SYSTEM SHALL use the effective instant of each match for every cutoff decision and every stored instant, so that step instants, reveal instants and application instants of a match never decrease, also across restarts and backward clock jumps; after a backward jump, steps wait until the effective instant reaches their scheduled instant (D16).
- FR-250: THE SYSTEM SHALL check, at server start for every running match and before every step, that the map package listed under the match's map id exists, passes validation and has the stored map digest; IF it does not, THEN THE SYSTEM SHALL halt the match with reason `map-changed`; IF the engine reports `isolated-faction` at a phase-1 close, THEN THE SYSTEM SHALL halt it with reason `isolated-faction` (P21, D17).
- FR-251: WHEN a match is halted, THE SYSTEM SHALL: (a) store the stage `halted` and the reason, and publish a `match-halted` event (round, reason); (b) perform no further step and never reveal the match secret; (c) leave unscored the stored predictions of the halting round that were not yet scored (victim predictions when halted at or before its phase-1 close; absorber predictions when halted after it; victim predictions already scored stay scored) and every winner prediction; (d) keep the scored results and leaderboard as they are; (e) keep in the public data the FR-288 fields when the halting round's victim was revealed; (f) reply `match-halted` to predictions and advances, serve every read of FR-281 as for a running match with the stage `halted` and the reason; (g) not count the match toward the running-match limit (D17).

### Secrecy and anti-spoiler (D5, D14)
- FR-252: THE SYSTEM SHALL draw each match secret as 256 bits from a cryptographic random source, and SHALL derive the round seed s_k from the match secret and k only, as the first 32 bits (big-endian) of HMAC-SHA-256 keyed with the secret over a message naming k; the message format is a published verification rule, fixed in the plan and never changed for stored matches (D5 note).
- FR-253: THE SYSTEM SHALL NOT send to any client, write to any log, or include in any error, event or reply the match secret or any round seed, except the match secret of a finished match (FR-259).
- FR-254: THE SYSTEM SHALL send to clients only public data: before the phase-1 close of round k nothing about round k; between the phase-1 close and the application only the FR-288 fields and the phase-1 results of round k (FR-275), all computable from public data once the victim is known; no absorber, record, state or phase-2 result of round k before its application; nothing about any later round.
- FR-255: THE SYSTEM SHALL compute round seeds and round steps only when performing a phase-1 close or an application (FR-241, FR-243): it SHALL NOT compute, store, cache or log any round seed, victim, absorber, state or record of a round whose phase 1 has not closed.
- FR-256: THE SYSTEM SHALL NOT store, send or log the absorber or the state of the pending round before its application; any value of them held while computing the victim SHALL be discarded after the computation (P26; defence in depth under D14).
- FR-257: THE SYSTEM SHALL make the bytes of every reply, event and error independent of everything not yet public: for two matches with the same public data at a moment and different unrevealed outcomes (different victim before the phase-1 close; same victim and different absorber or later rounds before the application), the same requests SHALL get byte-identical replies and events apart from the match id, host token, commitment and reported time. Design constraint (verified by NFR-47): replies and events are built only from a public view of the match that has no unrevealed field, so their timing cannot depend on one. No response-time bound is required.
- FR-258: IF a client asks for the state, record or any not-scored results of a round j with j > N (whether j ≤ T or j > T), THEN THE SYSTEM SHALL reply `round-not-applied` naming only N, the same reply for every such j and every stage; the only data of the pending round available are the FR-288 fields (public data and events) and, once phase 1 is scored, its phase-1 results (FR-275).
- FR-259: WHEN a match is created, THE SYSTEM SHALL publish its commitment; WHEN a match is finished (never when halted), THE SYSTEM SHALL publish its match secret and the derivation rule, so that anyone can check the commitment and recompute every round seed and every round with the engine.
- FR-260: THE SYSTEM SHALL allow a test-only round-seed source (replacing FR-252's derivation for tests) only as a parameter of the in-process server construction used by tests; no environment variable, setting, request or production entry point SHALL be able to set it.

### Predictions (D2, D8)
- FR-261: THE SYSTEM SHALL accept prediction submissions of three kinds, each for one match and one player id: **victim** for the pending round in stage `phase-1`, **absorber** for the pending round in stage `phase-2`, **winner** for the match until its cutoff.
- FR-262: IF a victim prediction does not name a faction living at N, THEN THE SYSTEM SHALL reject it with `invalid-choice`.
- FR-263: IF an absorber prediction does not name an absorption candidate of the revealed victim in the state at N (as the engine computes it, FR-313(b)), THEN THE SYSTEM SHALL reject it with `invalid-choice`.
- FR-264: IF a winner prediction does not name a winner candidate at the effective instant it is received, THEN THE SYSTEM SHALL reject it with `invalid-choice`.
- FR-265: THE SYSTEM SHALL validate every prediction against public data only (FR-257).
- FR-266: IF a submission reaches the server at an effective instant at or after its cutoff, or in a stage in which its kind is not open (an absorber prediction in `phase-1` or `locked`, a victim prediction in `phase-2` or `locked`, a per-round prediction naming a round other than the pending one, a winner prediction after the phase-1 close of round T), THEN THE SYSTEM SHALL reject it with `prediction-closed` and store nothing; on a finished match with `match-finished`, on a halted match with `match-halted`; the decision SHALL use the server's effective instant only, never a time sent by the client.
- FR-267: THE SYSTEM SHALL keep at most one prediction per (match, player id, kind, round) for victim and absorber, and one per (match, player id) for winner; a valid submission before the cutoff SHALL replace the stored one (P11) and increase its version by one; a submission equal to the stored one SHALL change nothing; both reply with the stored prediction and its version. Versions are informational only: there is no expected-version check, so a delayed older resubmission without a client request id that arrives last overwrites a newer one (P37). For a winner prediction, THE SYSTEM SHALL store the number of winner candidates at the effective instant of each change.
- FR-268: THE SYSTEM SHALL let a player predict either phase of a round, both or neither, and the winner independently; a missed cutoff gives no prediction and no points for that phase, and no penalty.
- FR-269: WHEN a phase closes with no prediction stored for it, THE SYSTEM SHALL continue the match exactly as scheduled, with an empty result for that phase.
- FR-270: THE SYSTEM SHALL let a client, with its client credential, read the stored predictions of its own player ids in one match (choices, versions, rounds); THE SYSTEM SHALL NOT publish any other player's prediction, nor any count or share of predictions, for a phase that is not scored (P12).

### Scoring and results (D12, D4)
- FR-271: WHEN phase 1 of round k closes, THE SYSTEM SHALL score every stored victim prediction of round k: correct if it names the victim, worth n points, where n is the number of factions living at N (k − 1); otherwise 0.
- FR-272: WHEN round k is applied, THE SYSTEM SHALL score every stored absorber prediction of round k: correct if it names the absorber, worth d points, where d is the number of absorption candidates of the victim in the state at k − 1 (P27); otherwise 0.
- FR-273: WHEN the match is finished, THE SYSTEM SHALL score every winner prediction: correct if it names the winner, worth the number of winner candidates stored at its last change (P27); otherwise 0.
- FR-274: THE SYSTEM SHALL give no streak bonus and no negative points.
- FR-275: THE SYSTEM SHALL let a client read, without any credential, the results of a scored phase of round k: the victim (phase 1) or the record of round k (phase 2) and, for every player id that predicted that phase, the choice, correctness and points, ordered by player id, paged (FR-279); asking for the results of a phase that is not scored replies `round-not-applied` naming only N (FR-258).
- FR-276: THE SYSTEM SHALL let a client read, without any credential, the results of one player id in one match: per scored phase, the choice, correctness and points; the winner prediction's correctness and points once finished; and the total, paged (FR-279).
- FR-277: THE SYSTEM SHALL let a client read, without any credential, the leaderboard of a match: one row per player id with at least one scored prediction, including players whose scored predictions are all worth 0 (D23), with the total points, the number of correct predictions per kind and the rank; ranked by total points, equal totals sharing a rank (1, 1, 3), then listed by player id; paged (FR-279).
- FR-278: THE SYSTEM SHALL treat player ids as opaque: it SHALL NOT parse, resolve or display them as names; mapping player ids to Discord users is step 4.
- FR-279: THE SYSTEM SHALL page every list read (match list, records, phase results, player results, leaderboard, own predictions) the same way: (a) a page size from 1 to 500 (default 100) and an opaque cursor returned with each page that is not the last; (b) a fixed order key per list (match list: creation instant, then match id; leaderboard: rank, then player id; records: round; the others: player id, or round then kind); (c) following cursors never repeats or skips an item that existed when the first page was read; (d) IF the leaderboard has changed since its first page (a new phase was scored), THEN THE SYSTEM SHALL reply `stale-cursor` and the client starts again (P38); (e) a page size outside 1 to 500 or a cursor the server did not issue for that list is rejected with `invalid-request`.

### Interface, reads, events and credentials (D9, D10)
- FR-280: THE SYSTEM SHALL offer commands and reads as HTTP requests with JSON bodies, and live events as a Server-Sent Events stream per match, with no third-party library; IF a request names no known path, THEN THE SYSTEM SHALL reply `unknown-path`; IF a known path is called with a method it does not offer, THEN `method-not-allowed`.
- FR-281: THE SYSTEM SHALL offer exactly these reads (P34): (a) the match list (FR-279); (b) a match's public data; (c) for each applied round j (0 ≤ j ≤ N), the state at j with its state digest, and the record of round j (j ≥ 1); (d) the records 1..N in round order (paged); (e) the results of a scored phase (FR-275); (f) a player's results (FR-276); (g) the leaderboard (FR-277); (h) a client's own predictions (FR-270, credential required); (i) the event stream (FR-289); (j) the three files of each map package of the map list, by map id, exactly as stored, so that the viewer loads the map from the server. A round segment that is not a whole number ≥ 0 is rejected with `invalid-request`; a valid round above N with `round-not-applied` (FR-258); an unknown match id with `unknown-match`; an unknown map id with `unknown-map`.
- FR-282: WHERE a command (creation, advance, submission) carries a client request id in its JSON body (1 to 64 characters of the FR-297 set), THE SYSTEM SHALL: (a) memoise the reply under the scope (credential, match id), or (operator credential, "creation") for creations, for 24 hours after its first use; (b) for a repeated request with the same scope, request id and same content, return the memoised reply again (also after a cutoff) without applying it twice; (c) reject the same scope and request id with a different content with `request-id-reused`; (d) not memoise the replies `rate-limited`, `unknown-path`, `method-not-allowed`, `too-large`, `invalid-request`, `invalid-player-id`, `forbidden`, `unknown-match` and `unavailable` (the request may be retried with the same id; `unknown-match` added by D26); (e) after the retention, treat the id as new.
- FR-283: THE SYSTEM SHALL read credentials only from the HTTP `Authorization` header with the `Bearer` scheme, never from the address or the body (P22), SHALL refuse to start if any configured operator or client credential is outside the credential character set and length or has fewer than 128 bits of randomness (at least 22 base64url or 32 hexadecimal characters), or if two client credentials share a namespace, and SHALL accept namespaces of 1 to 16 characters of `a-z`, `0-9`.
- FR-284: THE SYSTEM SHALL require a client credential for every prediction submission and every read of own predictions, and SHALL build the player id as `<namespace of the credential>:<local player id>`; a local player id that contains a namespace separator or any character outside FR-297 SHALL be rejected with `invalid-player-id` (P31): the namespace comes from the credential only.
- FR-285: THE SYSTEM SHALL let anyone, without any credential, use every read of FR-281 except own predictions, including the event stream (it carries only public data, and a browser event source cannot set headers, P22).
- FR-286: THE SYSTEM SHALL answer cross-origin browser requests as follows: credential-free reads, map files and the event stream for any origin, without credentials; requests carrying the `Authorization` header, and their preflight requests, only for the origins of the allowed-origins server setting (empty by default); other origins get no cross-origin permission.
- FR-287: THE SYSTEM SHALL push to subscribers these events, in this order within one step: (a) `phase-closed` (k, phase, step instant); (b) `victim-revealed` (FR-288); (c) `round-applied` (k, record of round k, state digest of round k, application instant: the step instant, which is the absorber reveal instant, for every round including T); (d) `results` (k, phase: available for reading); (e) `schedule` (stage, C1, C2, A of the pending round, reported time) after every step and every anchor shift of a running match, never after the application of round T; (f) `match-finished` (winner, match secret, derivation rule, reported time); (g) `match-halted` (round, reason, reported time).
- FR-288: THE SYSTEM SHALL include in every `victim-revealed` event: k, the victim, the absorption candidates of the victim in the state at N, the factions living at N in faction order (the victim included), the reveal instant (the instant of the stored phase-1 close step), C2, A, the reported time and the event's sequence number; and SHALL include the same fields in the public data while the stage is `phase-2` or `locked` (and `halted` per FR-251(e)), so that a viewer's panel and freshness check have them without any other request, also after joining or reconnecting late (hook for spec 009; whether an animation plays for them is governed by spec 009, under which late joiners and reloads get the final state with no tour) (D20).
- FR-289: THE SYSTEM SHALL number and replay the events of a match as follows: (a) sequence numbers start at 1 and grow by one per event; every event is kept as long as the match is stored; (b) the public data carries the sequence number of the latest event of the match, in every stage, so that a client that reads the public data and then subscribes with that number misses nothing; (c) the first event of a match is a `schedule` event (stage `phase-1`, anchors when the clock runs), stored with the creation; (d) WHEN a client subscribes with a last sequence number, given in the standard `Last-Event-ID` header or, for a first connection, in an address parameter, THE SYSTEM SHALL send every later event of the match in order, without gaps or duplicates; a number above the latest gives no events and no error; (e) a subscription without a number receives only events published after it is opened (live only); (f) THE SYSTEM SHALL send a comment line at least every 15 s on an idle stream (D23), which carries no data and consumes no sequence number.
- FR-290: THE SYSTEM SHALL publish an event only after the data it announces is stored, and SHALL publish no event about anything not yet public (FR-254).

### Persistence, recovery and concurrency (D7, D16)
- FR-291: THE SYSTEM SHALL store matches, predictions, steps, results, events and memoised replies in one SQLite database file through the platform's built-in SQLite module, behind a storage interface that the pure logic does not import; one server process SHALL hold an exclusive lock on the database, and a second process SHALL refuse to start (`database-locked`); THE SYSTEM SHALL store a schema version and refuse to start on an unknown version (migrations, if any, are defined by later plans).
- FR-292: THE SYSTEM SHALL store every accepted prediction before replying that it is accepted, and every step with its results before publishing it, durably (a stored item survives a process kill or power loss after the reply); IF a storage write fails, THEN THE SYSTEM SHALL reply `unavailable`, report nothing as accepted or performed, and retry a failed step every 3 s, the retried step following FR-248 once it is overdue.
- FR-293: THE SYSTEM SHALL store, for recovery, per match: the timing setting, map id and map digest, creation instant, stage, N, the schedule anchors t0, C1, C2 and A of the pending round as currently shifted, the last step instant, the revealed victim and its reveal instant during `phase-2` and `locked`, the halt reason; and per step row: round, kind, instant, cause, and the sequence numbers of its events (D16).
- FR-294: WHEN the server starts with stored matches, THE SYSTEM SHALL resume each running match at its stored stage, round and anchors, never performing a stored step again and never skipping one, then apply FR-250 and FR-248; the resumed match SHALL give the same states, records and results as a match that never stopped, with the same predictions; events stored but not yet sent SHALL be available with their original sequence numbers.
- FR-295: THE SYSTEM SHALL decide the submissions, advances and clock or late steps of one match one at a time, in the order the server receives them (a step trigger counts as received at its effective instant), each seeing the stored result of the ones before (R4): (a) a prediction counts for its phase if and only if it was stored before the phase's cutoff and before the closing step began; (b) an advance names the step it expects (round and kind); IF that step is the next step, THEN it is performed (FR-244) and the reply carries the step performed (round, kind, instant, cause) and the public data after it; IF that step was already performed (by the clock, a late catch-up or an earlier advance), THEN nothing is performed and the reply carries that step's row with `already-performed` and the current public data; IF it names any other step, THEN the reply is `step-mismatch` with the current stage and N; (c) so two advances naming the same step perform one step, and an advance that loses a race with the clock performs nothing.

### Input validation and abuse limits (D15)
- FR-296: THE SYSTEM SHALL read every request body as untrusted data with the own-property rule: unknown fields, missing fields, values that are not of the required JSON type and texts outside their limits are rejected with `invalid-request` naming the first failing field in the documented field order, and nothing is stored; numeric range rules of timing values are `invalid-timing` (FR-233).
- FR-297: THE SYSTEM SHALL accept a local player id or client request id only if it is 1 to 64 characters, each in `A-Z`, `a-z`, `0-9`, `_`, `-`, `.`; a local player id outside this set is rejected `invalid-player-id`, a request id `invalid-request`.
- FR-298: IF a request body exceeds 4 KiB (P16), THEN THE SYSTEM SHALL reject it with `too-large` without reading further.
- FR-299: THE SYSTEM SHALL apply rate limits (D15) as follows: (a) per credential (each client credential, the operator credential, each host token) and, for requests without a valid credential, per network address; (b) with values from the server settings, defaults (P16): per client credential 600 commands and 1,200 reads per minute and 50 open event streams; per player id per match 30 submissions per minute; per host token 30 advances per minute; per network address without credential 120 requests per minute and 5 open event streams; (c) each per-minute limit counts the accepted (not rate-limited) requests received in the last 60,000 ms (sliding window); (d) limit state is kept in memory only and need not survive a restart; (e) IF one or more limits are exceeded, THEN THE SYSTEM SHALL reply `rate-limited` with the whole number of seconds (at least 1) until every exceeded limit admits the request again (the largest wait), also as the HTTP `Retry-After` value, and store nothing; (f) a test setting raises every limit for tests and the NFR-49 measurement.
- FR-300: THE SYSTEM SHALL compare credentials and host tokens in time independent of their content and store host tokens as one-way digests (apart from the FR-238 memo).
- FR-301: THE SYSTEM SHALL report errors as English `kind` identifiers, one per reply, the first applicable in this fixed order (P33): `rate-limited`, `unknown-path`, `method-not-allowed`, `too-large`, `invalid-request`, `invalid-player-id`, `forbidden`, `request-id-reused`, `unknown-match`, `unknown-map`, map validation errors (001 order), `too-few-factions`, `invalid-timing`, `too-many-matches`, `match-halted`, `match-finished`, `step-mismatch`, `round-not-applied`, `stale-cursor`, `prediction-closed`, `invalid-choice`, `unavailable`. Only the map validation errors and `invalid-timing` (each failed rule) may list several items; no error SHALL echo a secret, credential, token or round seed. Halt reasons (`map-changed`, `isolated-faction`, `victim-mismatch`) and the advance result `already-performed` are data, not errors.
- FR-302: THE SYSTEM SHALL send the reported time with every reply and event that carries a schedule or match state, so that clients show countdowns and ages without deciding any cutoff.

### Clients: live viewer, web test client, debug scene
- FR-303: WHEN the viewer is opened on a live match (match id in the page address, P20), THE SYSTEM SHALL load the map package from the server (FR-281(j)), show the state at the last applied round N received from the server, SHALL ask the server only for rounds 0..N, and WHEN a `round-applied` event for N + 1 arrives SHALL play the 005 round step to it; it MAY first play the spec-009 absorber tour over the old state, so that the colour change starts up to about 2.5 s later, while the displayed round number, the panel and the page address change at once (D20); it SHALL hold no data it has not received.
- FR-304: WHILE showing a live match, THE SYSTEM SHALL NOT run the engine's round step or any draw of the engine (round or seed) in the browser; presentation-only cosmetic generators are allowed and SHALL NOT depend on the match secret (D20); it MAY build the state at N + 1 by applying a received record to the held state at N (transfer of the listed territories to the absorber), and IF the state digest of the result differs from the event's, THEN SHALL read the state from the server instead; it SHALL give the rendering part only the data of 004 FR-159 and 005 FR-188, plus the presentation data that spec 009 specifies (dim factor, undimmed factions, marker, edge indicator), which SHALL carry no unrevealed information; stepping backwards over received rounds stays allowed.
- FR-305: THE SYSTEM SHALL show in the live viewer's panel, as Spanish text: the stage and the countdown to the next scheduled step in every running stage (none with timing `manual`); during `phase-2` and `locked`, the victim's name once the spec-009 reveal animation stops on it (a Spanish text meaning "revealing…" meanwhile), or at once when no animation plays (for example reduced motion, late join, a stale event) (D19); the winner when finished; a halted notice with the reason when halted (the map keeps showing the state at N; marking the victim on the map is spec 009).
- FR-306: THE SYSTEM SHALL decide the viewer's scene from the page address: (a) both a seed and a match id: a Spanish error and no scene, in every build; (b) a match id: the live match; (c) a seed only: the debug scene in development builds, and in production builds a Spanish message that a match id is needed, with no scene; (d) neither: in development builds the debug scene as today (004), in production builds the same Spanish message, with no scene.
- FR-307: IF the server is unreachable, THEN THE SYSTEM SHALL keep the last drawn state, show a Spanish notice and retry after 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, then every 30 s; IF the match id is unknown, THEN THE SYSTEM SHALL show a Spanish error and no scene; WHEN the event stream drops, THE SYSTEM SHALL reconnect with the last sequence number received (FR-289(d)) and apply the missed events in order; on first connection it SHALL subscribe with the last event sequence number of the public data it read (FR-289(b)).
- FR-308: THE SYSTEM SHALL compute the map digest in the viewer over the raw bytes of the three files as downloaded, before any decoding, and IF the result differs from the match's map digest, THEN SHALL show a Spanish error and draw no state; IF the browser's built-in digest function is unavailable (it exists only on secure origins: `https` or `localhost`; for example a plain `http` LAN address), THEN SHALL show a Spanish error that names the need for a secure address and draw no state (fail closed).
- FR-309: THE SYSTEM SHALL keep the debug scene as it is for locally chosen seeds in development builds (except the offline-only address parameter `reveal` that spec 009 adds, D20), SHALL NOT connect it to the server or offer it for a live match, and SHALL leave it out of production builds (004 FR-161, decision Q5).
- FR-310: THE SYSTEM SHALL provide, in development builds only (P28), a web test client in which a tester types a local player id and submits the victim, absorber and winner predictions of a live match through the `web` client credential, and sees the stage, the countdown, the revealed victim, the stored predictions and the results; every visible text comes from the UI strings module, in Spanish.
- FR-311: THE SYSTEM SHALL produce production build output that contains neither the web test client nor any client credential value (checked by a test over the build output with a planted credential).

### Determinism and the single engine
- FR-312: THE SYSTEM SHALL keep the engine's existing single-seed functions unchanged, and the new round step (FR-313(a)) chained from round 0 with every round seed equal to one seed SHALL equal `getState` and `getRounds` for that seed (D6).
- FR-313: THE SYSTEM SHALL provide in the sim package (D6, D21, P35) three exports, each with every input typed `unknown` and read with the own-property rule, and its errors reported together in the 001 FR-47 style, in this order: map validation errors, `invalid-state`, then `invalid-seed` (step) or `invalid-faction` (candidates):
  - (a) a round step taking (map, state, round seed) and returning `Result<{ state: MatchState; record: RoundRecord }, MatchError>`: the state is valid when its mode is `classic`, its round a whole number from 0 to T and its owners exactly the map's land territories in map order, each owned by a faction of the map (else `invalid-state`); the round seed is checked as 001 FR-31 (`invalid-seed`); IF the state's round is T or the state has fewer than 2 living factions, THEN it returns `round-out-of-range` with round = state round + 1 and total = T; with a victim that has no candidate it returns `isolated-faction` as today; otherwise round state round + 1 drawn from `roundRng(roundSeed, state round + 1)` exactly as the internal step;
  - (b) the absorption candidates taking (map, state, faction) and returning `Result<FactionId[], MatchError>`: the candidate factions of the internal candidate operation (002 FR-81), in its order, without its internal `how` field; a faction id that is not a faction of the map gives `invalid-faction`; a faction of the map that owns no territory gives an empty list;
  - (c) the living factions taking (map, state) and returning `Result<FactionId[], MatchError>`: the factions owning at least one territory, in faction order of the state, exactly as the internal function.
- FR-314: THE SYSTEM SHALL read time only through an injectable clock and draw secrets, ids and tokens only through an injectable random source, both in the server layer; shared and sim stay pure (constitution 3, 001 NFR-1).
- FR-315: THE SYSTEM SHALL compute every round of a live match only with the engine (no second implementation of the rules), and for a fixed match secret and map SHALL give the same victims, states and records whatever the timing setting, the instants of steps, manual advances, late steps, restarts and predictions.

## Non-functional requirements
- NFR-47 (Anti-spoiler, verified): (a) tests with the test-only round-seed source (FR-260) build matches with the same public data and different unrevealed outcomes (different victim before the phase-1 close; same victim, different absorber during phase 2) and check FR-257 on every read, command and event; (b) a type-level and structure test checks the FR-257 design constraint: reply and event builders take only the public view type, which has no field for an absorber, pending state, round seed or secret; (c) a scan with planted known secrets and round seeds checks that no reply, event or log line before the end of the match contains them in decimal, hexadecimal (both cases), base64, base64url or raw bytes; (d) a storage check after each step finds no absorber or state of the pending round and nothing of a later round (FR-255, FR-256); (e) a statistical test on the dev map over 2^20 round seeds checks that the round-1 absorber, given the victim, is spread over the candidates within ±2 percentage points of uniform; (f) a test checks that the production entry point offers no way to set the round-seed source (FR-260).
- NFR-48 (Determinism, golden): golden files pin, for fixed match secrets on the dev map, every round seed, victim, record and state digest, the map digest of `maps/dev`, and the results and leaderboard of a fixed set of predictions; the same golden results come out with timing `manual` and with several clock settings (including L2 = 0 and L2 > 0), with any fake-clock schedule, with manual advances in every stage (a phase-2 advance closing phase 2 only and the application following L2 later), with late steps and backward clock jumps, and with a restart after every step.
- NFR-49 (Performance, P17): reference setup: the user's Windows 11 development PC (004 NFR-24), the pinned Node version, server and load script on the same machine, a file database on the local disk, the dev map, 10 running matches, limits raised (FR-299(f)); 1,000 open event streams spread over the matches (100 each); 100 submissions per second from 1,000 player ids through one client credential, measured over 60 s after 10 s of warm-up. Targets: each step performed and its events sent to every stream within 100 ms of its instant; a prediction reply within 50 ms at the 95th percentile; restart with 100 stored matches ready within 2 s. Measured by a script, figures in the pull request, not in CI.
- NFR-50 (Clock accuracy): while the server runs and its clock does not jump, a scheduled step takes place no earlier than its instant and at most 1,000 ms after it, verified with the fake clock (logic) and by measurement (real clock); host settings with windows shorter than this tolerance are allowed (D11) and behave within it.
- NFR-51 (Purity): validation, the timing check, the schedule, anchors and stage machine, the late-step, effective-instant and reported-time rules, the advance decision of FR-295, scoring, ranking, paging order and the digests' canonical forms are pure functions with time, state and inputs as parameters, tested without a network, a disk or the real clock; the 001 purity scans of shared and sim stay green.
- NFR-52 (Tests with what exists): Vitest only, no extra test or mocking library (constitution 4); unit tests of the pure parts; integration tests (from M2 for the store and lifecycle, from M3 for the network) that run the server in-process with a fake clock, an in-memory SQLite database and real HTTP requests on a local port, reading event streams with a small hand-written reader over the platform's `fetch` (no event-source or mocking library); one test with a file database across a restart.
- NFR-53 (Dependencies): no new third-party dependency (D7, D9); shared and sim keep none; the results of the D7 checks are recorded in the plan.
- NFR-54 (Privacy and logging): the store holds only namespaced opaque player ids, choices, versions, client request ids, memoised replies (including, for 24 hours, the plain host token of a creation reply, FR-238) and instants about players; logs contain no match secret, round seed, credential, token, player id or prediction choice (constitution 5); the database file and secrets are never committed (ignored by version control).
- NFR-55 (No regression and traceability): 001 to 007 tests pass with the meaning of their assertions, except the export-list assertions that this spec changes by decision (D21): `packages/sim/test/candidates.test.ts` "FR-81 the operation is internal: not exported from @mbrg/sim" (lines 86 to 88: the internal `absorptionCandidates` stays unexported, but the exact export list grows), `packages/sim/test/match.test.ts` "plan 6 export list is exact" (line 146) and `packages/shared/test/no-regression.test.ts` NFR-22 (lines 30 to 33); each is updated to the new exact list (the five existing names plus the three exports of FR-313) and keeps asserting that `absorptionCandidates` itself is not exported; golden files and the dev map are byte-identical; `SimError` grows only by `invalid-state` and `invalid-faction`; every FR and NFR id of this spec appears in a test title; the traceability test expects ids up to FR-315 and NFR-57.
- NFR-56 (Language): code, comments, docs and error kinds in English; the viewer's and the web test client's visible texts are Spanish, in the UI strings module.
- NFR-57 (Durability and recovery, verified): an integration test kills the server between every pair of storage transactions of a scripted match (in-process abort over a file database) and checks after restart that no accepted prediction is lost, no step is repeated or skipped, and the stored data equal those of an uninterrupted run: in M2 the states, records, steps and predictions (results stored empty); from M4 also the results and leaderboard (golden of NFR-48).

## Edge cases
- Submission at the exact cutoff instant: rejected (exclusive cutoff, FR-266).
- Submission that arrives while a closing step is being stored: rejected `prediction-closed` (FR-295(a)).
- An absorber prediction before the victim is revealed, or a victim prediction after: rejected `prediction-closed` (FR-266).
- Prediction to a finished match: `match-finished`; to a halted match: `match-halted` (FR-266).
- Manual advance with the clock on, a moment before C1: phase 1 closes now; C2 = now + L1 (FR-244(a)); the clock does not close phase 1 again at the old C1.
- Manual advance in phase 2: only the phase-2 close; the stage becomes `locked` and the application follows L2 later (FR-244(b), D22); with L2 = 0 the application falls due at once (FR-240).
- Two manual advances naming the same step at the same time: one step; the second gets `already-performed` (FR-295(b)). An advance naming phase-1 close of round k while the clock just performed it: `already-performed`, nothing more happens.
- An advance naming a step two steps ahead: `step-mismatch` (FR-295(b)).
- L2 = 0: no `locked` stage; two step rows at the same instant, events in the FR-287 order.
- Timing `manual`: three advances per round; nothing happens while nobody advances; no countdown is shown.
- Very short host settings (for example I = 3, L1 = 1, L2 = 0): accepted (D11); steps fall within the 1,000 ms tolerance of NFR-50.
- A creation with I = 3.5: `invalid-timing`; with I given as text "3600" or missing: `invalid-request` (FR-233).
- T = 0 (one faction): creation rejected `too-few-factions`. T = 1: one round; the phase-1 reveal decides the winner, so winner predictions close then (FR-237).
- The victim has exactly one absorption candidate (d = 1): phase 2 still runs; the only valid choice is worth 1 point (FR-272).
- A winner prediction naming the revealed victim during phase 2: rejected `invalid-choice` (FR-264); a stored winner prediction for a faction later eliminated scores 0.
- No prediction at all in a phase or a whole match: the match runs as scheduled; empty results, empty leaderboard (FR-269).
- Server down across C1 (even across C1, C2 and A): at start only the phase-1 close is performed, late, at the start instant (only predictions stored before C1 count); a full phase 2 and the locked wait follow (FR-248).
- Server down across C2 (stage `phase-2`, whether or not A also passed): at start only the phase-2 close is performed, late, at the start instant (only predictions stored before C2 count); the application follows L2 later (FR-248, D22).
- Server down across A only (stage `locked`): at start the round is applied, late; round k + 1 is anchored at the start instant.
- A storage failure at C1, retried every 3 s: once overdue, the step is performed late and phase 2 gets its full L1 from that instant (FR-248, FR-292).
- The clock jumps forward one hour: the next step is overdue and performed late at the new instant; the round is not compressed (FR-248).
- The clock jumps backward one hour: the effective instant stays at the last step instant; steps and cutoffs wait until the clock catches up, so the round lasts up to one hour longer; reported times never decrease (FR-249, Definitions).
- Server down after storing a step but before sending its events: they are available at restart with their original sequence numbers (FR-294).
- A viewer reads the public data (last event number 41) and subscribes with 41: it misses nothing; a viewer subscribing without a number gets only live events (FR-289).
- Map package replaced on disk: at the next start or step the match halts with `map-changed` (FR-250); unscored predictions of the halting round stay unscored; the secret is not revealed (FR-251).
- Halt at the application (`victim-mismatch`): the victim predictions of that round stay scored, the absorber predictions unscored; the public data keeps the victim fields (FR-251).
- A viewer that asks for round N + 1, N + 2 or T + 5: the same `round-not-applied` (N); round `-1` or `x`: `invalid-request` (FR-281).
- A local player id `discord:123` sent by the `web` client: rejected `invalid-player-id` (FR-284).
- A bot serving many players from one address: limited per its credential and per player id, not per address (FR-299).
- Retrying an accepted submission after the cutoff with the same client request id: the memoised reply (FR-282). Retrying after `rate-limited` with the same id: processed as new.
- Two clients of one namespace use the same local id: they write the same player's predictions; last valid write wins (FR-267).
- A paged leaderboard read across a phase close: `stale-cursor`, start again (FR-279).
- A second server process on the same database: refuses to start (FR-291).
- The viewer opened on `http://192.168.x.x` (insecure origin): a Spanish error about the secure address, no state (FR-308).

## Decisions
Decided by the user (chat answers, 2026-10-21).
- D1. **Match lifecycle: both a server clock and a manual advance.** Predictions close before what they are about is revealed. (Timing made precise by D8, D11, D16 and D22; who may advance by D10.)
- D2. **Prediction kinds: all three**: who is eliminated, which neighbour absorbs the eliminated faction, who wins the match. Further kinds (OP1 to OP5) are out of 008 (D13).
- D3. **Persistence: a SQLite file**, behind a small storage interface so the engine stays pure. The library is D7.
- D4. **Identity: an opaque external player id supplied by the client**: no accounts, no passwords, no personal data stored. The server offers per-phase and final results per player id and a leaderboard; mapping to Discord users is step 4. (Namespacing and client credentials: D10.)
- D5. **Secrecy (approves P1 and P3)**: 256-bit match secret; per-round seeds by HMAC-SHA-256 with the platform's crypto (no new dependency); the SHA-256 of the secret published at creation; the secret revealed at the end. Discarded: a single 32-bit seed (brute-forced from past rounds on large maps); a 128-bit engine seed; fresh stored seeds without commitment. Note for the user: the exact HMAC message format of FR-252 is a published verification rule; the plan fixes it once, and it cannot change for stored matches.
- D6. **Two sim exports approved (P2)** as behaviour-neutral additions: the one-round step with a given round seed, and the absorption candidates for a state; chaining equals `getState` / `getRounds`. (Extended by D21.)
- D7. **Storage: the built-in `node:sqlite`** (no third-party dependency), provided it works without a flag on the Node version the repository pins; the checks and the fallback are open checks for the plan.
- D8. **Two phases per round (replaces P7)**: phase 1 predicts the victim, with its own cutoff; at its close the victim is revealed; phase 2 predicts which neighbour absorbs the victim's territory, with a second cutoff before the round is applied. The victim is never revealed before the phase-1 cutoff, the absorber never before the phase-2 cutoff. Discarded: a joint (victim, absorber) pair; "any absorber this round".
- D9. **Interface: HTTP + Server-Sent Events** (approves P9), no new library.
- D10. **Identity and authority**: one credential per client, player ids namespaced per client (`web:`, `discord:`); creating a match needs the operator credential from the environment; a manual advance needs the operator credential or the per-match host token returned once at creation.
- D11. **Host-configured timing with validation (replaces P4, P5, P8)**: the host sets the interval I and the leads of both phases per match before the match starts; the server only rejects impossible or absurd settings; everything else is the host's choice. Any default exists only as a client-side proposal (P25).
- D12. **Scoring (approves P14 and P10)**: inverse probability: victim = number of living factions at the time; absorber = number of absorption candidates of the victim; no streaks; ties share rank (1, 1, 3); the winner prediction stays open until round T and is worth the number of living factions when it was last changed.
- D13. **Every remaining planner proposal approved as recommended: P11 to P29**, and **no extra prediction kinds in 008**: OP1 to OP5 stay out; OP5 ("X survives until round k") is the first candidate for a later spec.
- D14 (QA Q1). **Threat model**: the operator and anyone who can read the database file or backups are trusted; secrets are stored in plain form in the database; P26 (absorber never stored) is defence in depth only; encryption at rest is out of scope, a candidate for a later spec.
- D15 (QA Q2). **Rate limits per credential** (a bot has its own), as configurable operator settings with a test override (NFR-49 runs with limits raised); the address limit applies only to requests without a valid credential; window, restart behaviour and the `rate-limited` reply as in FR-299.
- D16 (QA Q3). **Late steps while running** (storage failure, stall, forward clock jump) follow the start-up catch-up rule: one overdue step as a manual advance at that instant, the rest of the round rescheduled from it, so players always get a full phase; reveal instants never decrease (effective instant rule); the stored anchors are listed in FR-293.
- D17 (QA Q4). **Halted matches**: the secret is not revealed; pending predictions of the halting round stay unscored; the leaderboard stays as is; halted matches do not count toward the running-match limit; predictions and advances reply `match-halted`, reads work; the map check runs at start and at every step.
- D18 (closes P30). **One spec 008 with the five milestones M1 to M5**, each with its own plan section and at most 10 tasks. Discarded: separate specs 008a to 008e.
- D19 (from spec 009). **Panel shows the victim's name when the reveal animation stops on it** ("revelando…" meanwhile), and at once when no animation plays (reduced motion, late join, stale event) (FR-305).
