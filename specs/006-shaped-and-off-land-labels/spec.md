# Spec 006 — Shaped and off-land labels
Status: approved

## Context and goal
Spec 005 (merged, PR #25) draws one name per faction and one per land territory, each as the **horizontal** box of largest height that fits the label's main region or main piece, with white text and a black 1 px halo, crossfaded by zoom and animated by the round-step transitions. Two kinds of places are labelled badly by that rule:
1. **Regions whose shape is not horizontal**: a tall strip, a diagonal peninsula or a curved coast. The horizontal box only uses the region's width, so the name comes out much smaller than the region allows (or not at all).
2. **Regions where no readable name fits inside the land**: small territories, thin slivers and islands. 005 draws them tiny or not at all (005 FR-170, FR-172).

This spec defines:
1. **Shaped labels**: besides the horizontal box of 005, a label may be drawn along a straight centre line rotated to follow the region's axis (vertical or diagonal) or along a circular arc that follows a bend of the region. A shaped label is used only when it is clearly larger than the horizontal one; otherwise the 005 box stays, unchanged.
2. **Off-land labels**: a label whose name does not fit inside its land at a readable size is placed next to the land, outside it, at the nearest free position, with a leader line when it had to move away.

Why: the user asked for it in the 005 amendment (2026-10-16): "Vertical/diagonal/curved labels and off-land labels go to a later spec 006; mobile later". Names that follow the region's shape stay as large as the region allows; names of small places become readable instead of disappearing. Old viewer lesson (MEMORY.md): its label layout needed six patch iterations, so every rule below states its inputs, its outputs and its tie order.

Decisions of the user (2026-10-18): spec 006 comes next; moving `rewrite` to `main` is postponed and is not part of this spec. The six design choices of this spec (curve kind, when a shaped label wins, vertical reading direction, off-land trigger and size, off-land position and leader line, debug counts) were answered by the user on 2026-10-18 and are recorded under "Decisions" (D1 to D6); two more were answered on 2026-10-19 after the QA review (D7, D8), and one after the review of the T8 screenshots (D9: `shapeGain` lowered from 1.5 to 1.25; amendment of 2026-10-19, made after T1 to T8 were implemented). Choices the QA review left open were made by the planner and are recorded as P1 to P13 for the user to see (P2 and P5 confirmed by the user on 2026-10-19); the requirements below state all of them as final.

## Users
- **Spectator** (indirect, later): reads every name, also on thin countries, islands and small provinces.
- **Developer / map author**: needs placements that are reproducible from the map and the owners, with stated tie rules.
- **Reviewer (the user)**: accepts the look from screenshots of fixed scenes.

## User stories
- US-15. As a spectator, I want a long or bent country's name to run along its shape so that it is as large and as readable as the country allows.
- US-16. As a spectator, I want the names of islands and tiny provinces shown next to them, clearly attached, so that no place on the map is nameless.
- US-17. As a developer, I want every shaped or off-land placement to follow a rule with explicit inputs and tie order so that the same map and owners always give the same labels and the layout does not need patch-by-patch tuning.

## Definitions
- Terms of specs 001 to 005 keep their meaning: **map unit, map coordinates, row-major order, inside (a rectangle), region, main region, main piece, label box, text aspect ratio (a), fits (005), label parameters (M, Hmax, P, L0, L1, Dt), faction / territory label opacity, opacity factor, transition, forward progress f, eliminated faction, absorber, rendering part, scale, fit scale**.
- **Label set**: the set of map units a label belongs to: the faction's main region (faction label) or the territory's main piece (territory label), at the state considered. A faction's other regions and a territory's other pieces are not part of it.
- **Role**: faction label or territory label (005).
- **Unit centre** of the map unit at whole coordinates (x, y): (x + 0.5, y + 0.5). Every lattice point (x + 0.5, y + 0.5) with whole x, y, also outside the id image, is a unit centre; positions outside the id image belong to no set.
- **Screen angle** θ of a direction: degrees, measured from the direction pointing right on the screen, positive counter-clockwise **as seen on screen** (map y grows downwards, so the direction at θ is (cos θ, −sin θ) in map coordinates). The **reading direction** of a label is the direction in which its text is read. Allowed reading directions: −90° < θ ≤ +90°; θ = +90° reads from bottom to top (decision D3); no label reads from top to bottom.
- **Wrapped angle**: an angle mapped into (−90°, +90°] by adding or subtracting 180° (so +105° becomes −75° and −90° becomes +90°); the reading direction flips by 180°, the line of the text stays the same.
- **Exact angle values**: the angle of every frame computed at a state (not the interpolated angles of FR-210, nor the tangents along an arc) is a whole multiple of `angleStep`; its sine and cosine are the constants of the table under "Number reference", never results of a trigonometric function of the language (NFR-39).
- **Axis angle** θ* of a label set: let (mx, my) be the mean of its unit centres and Sxx, Syy, Sxy the sums over its units of (x − mx)², (y − my)² and (x − mx)(−(y − my)) for unit centres (x, y). θ* is the screen angle of the major axis of that second-moment ellipse, rounded to the nearest multiple of `angleStep`, an exact half step rounded towards 0°, and wrapped into (−90°, +90°]. When Sxx = Syy and Sxy = 0, θ* = 0°. θ* is the exact mathematical value of this definition, decided as stated under "Number reference".
- **Axis extent** L of a label set: the largest minus the smallest projection of its unit centres on the direction θ* (formula under "Number reference").
- **Label frame**: where and how a label's text sits, one of three shapes, each with a whole-number height h (the font size, in map units) and a length w = h × a along its reading direction:
  - **Horizontal frame**: the label box of 005 (x0, y0 whole numbers; reading direction 0°).
  - **Straight frame**: a rectangle of length w along the reading direction θ and height h across it, centred at c, with θ ≠ 0°.
  - **Arc frame**: its **middle line** is a circular arc of length w and radius R whose midpoint is c and whose tangent at c has the screen angle θ (0° allowed). Its **bend** β is a signed angle whose magnitude is in `arcBends`: β is the turn the middle line would make over a length equal to the axis extent L, so R = L / |β| (β in radians) and the middle line turns by w / R radians (w × |β| / L in the units of β) over its length w (decision D8). The arc turns counter-clockwise as seen on screen while reading when β > 0 (a "∪" shape for θ = 0°) and clockwise when β < 0 ("∩"). Every tangent of the middle line between its two ends must be an allowed reading direction, and R − h/2 − M > 0; when L = 0 no arc frame exists.
  - For straight and arc frames, the centre c is a unit centre.
- **Centre line** of a frame: the line on which every glyph is vertically centred (the vertical-centre convention of 005: the text's middle, not its alphabetic baseline): for a horizontal frame the horizontal line y = y0 + h/2; for a straight frame the line through c in the reading direction θ; for an arc frame its middle line. The footprint extends h/2 on each side of the centre line (for an arc, radii R ± h/2).
- **Footprint** of a frame: the area its text covers: the box (horizontal), the rectangle (straight), or the annular sector of radii R − h/2 to R + h/2 spanning an arc length w at radius R (arc). The **grown footprint** adds M on every side: the box or rectangle grown by M on each side; for an arc, radii R − h/2 − M to R + h/2 + M and arc length w + 2M at radius R, that is the angular half-width φ = (w + 2M) / (2R) (P11). Membership of a point is decided by the formulas under "Number reference" (points on the edge belong to it).
- **Arc margin is approximate at the ends** (P11): the grown arc footprint is bounded by radial lines at ±φ, so the margin beyond each end of the text is M measured along the middle line, but less than M at the inner edge (about M × (R − h/2 − M) / R plus the angular part of the text there) and more at the outer edge; end glyphs, which are drawn as upright rectangles rotated to the tangent, may also poke out of the sector by a fraction of a glyph corner. "Frame fits" is decided by this grown footprint as defined, not by the drawn glyphs.
- **Frame fits** a label set: every unit centre that lies in the grown footprint belongs to the set. For horizontal frames this is exactly the "fits" of 005.
- **Shapes of a label set**: the horizontal shape; the straight shapes at the wrapped angles of θ*, θ* − `angleStep` and θ* + `angleStep`, except 0°; and the arc shapes with tangent angle θ* at the midpoint and each bend in `arcBends` with both signs. **Shape order**: straight before arc; among straight shapes θ*, then the wrapped θ* − `angleStep`, then the wrapped θ* + `angleStep`; among arc shapes the smaller |β| first, then β > 0 before β < 0.
- **Horizontal height** hH: the height of the 005 FR-169 box of the label set (undefined when no box fits). **Shaped height** hS: the largest whole h from 1 to Hmax for which some straight or arc frame of some shape of the set fits (undefined when none fits).
- **In-land frame** of a label: the frame chosen by FR-198 (horizontal or shaped); its height is hIn.
- **Off-land box**: a horizontal box of height `offLandHeight` of the label's role (x0, y0 whole numbers, w = h × a), placed outside its label set (FR-204).
- **Gap** of an off-land box: the Euclidean distance from the box (not grown) to the nearest unit centre of its label set. **Anchor unit**: that nearest unit (ties: the first in row-major order). **Set centre**: (mx, my) above.
- **Drawn frame** of a label at a state: its off-land box if one was placed (FR-203 to FR-205), otherwise its in-land frame if it has one (including the fallback of FR-206), otherwise none.
- **Conflict**: two labels conflict when some unit centre lies in both of their grown footprints (for an off-land box, its grown box).
- **Leader line**: a straight segment from the point of an off-land box nearest to its anchor unit's centre to that centre.
- **New label parameters** (values decided by the user, D1 to D5, D7). **Fixed** values are part of the requirements; **tunable** values start at the decided value and may only be adjusted within the stated range during the NFR-40 screenshot review, with the user's acceptance:
  - `angleStep`: 15°, fixed (D2; the table under "Number reference" is built for it).
  - `shapeGain` (G): 1.25, fixed (D2, value changed from 1.5 by D9). A shaped frame must be at least G times taller than the horizontal one to win. G × hH is computed in double precision; it is exact for every whole hH used here (1.25 = 5/4), so ceil(G × hH) is exact.
  - `arcBends`: {30°, 60°}, fixed (D1, D7).
  - `offLandHeight`: per role, in map units, tunable (D4): territory 5 (range 3 to 12), faction 18 (range 10 to 40). It is both the in-land height below which a label goes off-land and the height at which the off-land label is drawn. The only tunable parameter of this spec.
  - `offLandReach` (Dr): 24 map units, fixed (D5). The largest gap of an off-land box.
  - `leaderGap` (Gl): 6 map units, fixed (D5). The gap above which a leader line is drawn (M < Gl ≤ Dr holds).

### Number reference
The single reference for every number of the layout (NFR-39). It is the definition: brute-force tests and the cross-engine hash use these same formulas, and a point that lies on an edge "in exact arithmetic" is inside or outside as these formulas say.
- **Exact part (integers).** The axis angle θ* and the centre tie rule of FR-197 are decided exactly, with whole numbers only (arbitrary-precision integers wherever a value can exceed 2^53, which happens on the dev map). For θ*, with the whole coordinates (x, y) of the set's units (the 0.5 offset of the centres cancels) and n units: A = (n·Σx² − (Σx)²) − (n·Σy² − (Σy)²) and B = −2·(n·Σxy − Σx·Σy) (that is, A = n²(Sxx − Syy), B = 2n²Sxy). Twice the major-axis angle is the angle of the direction (A, B); θ* is half of it rounded as defined. Each rounding boundary of that doubled angle (±15°, ±45°, ±75°, ±105°, ±135°, ±165°, and ±180° for the wrap) is decided by the sign of B·cos φ − A·sin φ, which for these boundaries is a positive multiple of p + q·√3 with whole p and q; that sign is decided by the signs of p and q and by comparing p² with 3q². Exact ties can occur only at doubled angles ±45° and ±135° (θ = ±22.5°, ±67.5°) and go towards 0° (±15°, ±60°). For FR-197, centres are unit centres, so the squared distances to their mean are compared as whole numbers after multiplying by (2 × number of centres)².
- **Pinned floating-point part.** Every other quantity is computed in IEEE-754 double precision with the formulas below, evaluated in the written order (parentheses first, then left to right), using only +, −, ×, ÷, `Math.sqrt`, `Math.abs`, `Math.min`, `Math.max`, `Math.floor`, `Math.ceil` and the constant `Math.PI`; whole numbers below 2^53 (coordinates, heights, sums of coordinates) are exact. On Number (double) values, `**`, `Math.pow`, `Math.hypot`, trigonometric, exponential and logarithmic functions are not used (squares are products x × x). The ban does not apply to the exact part: arbitrary-precision integer (BigInt) arithmetic, including `*` and `**` with a BigInt exponent, is exact and allowed there.
- **Constants** (C = cos θ, S = sin θ for the angle of an instant frame; √k means `Math.sqrt(k)`; for −θ use (C, −S)):

| θ | C | S |
|---|---|---|
| 0° | 1 | 0 |
| 15° | (√6 + √2) / 4 | (√6 − √2) / 4 |
| 30° | √3 / 2 | 1 / 2 |
| 45° | √2 / 2 | √2 / 2 |
| 60° | 1 / 2 | √3 / 2 |
| 75° | (√6 − √2) / 4 | (√6 + √2) / 4 |
| 90° | 0 | 1 |

- **Projection and extent.** For a unit centre (px, py): t = (px × C) − (py × S); L = (max t) − (min t) over the set.
- **Straight fit.** For centre (cx, cy) and unit centre (px, py): dx = px − cx, dy = py − cy (exact); u = (dx × C) − (dy × S); v = (dx × S) + (dy × C); inside the grown rectangle iff `Math.abs(u)` ≤ ((h × a) / 2) + M and `Math.abs(v)` ≤ (h / 2) + M.
- **Arc frame.** b = (|β| × `Math.PI`) / 180 (β in degrees); R = L / b; σ = +1 for β > 0, −1 for β < 0; circle centre Ox = cx − σ × (R × S), Oy = cy − σ × (R × C). Existence: R − (h / 2) − M > 0, and with α = ((h × a) × |β|) / (2 × L) (degrees, half the turn of the middle line): θ − α > −90 and θ + α ≤ 90. Membership of a unit centre: vx = px − Ox, vy = py − Oy, r2 = (vx × vx) + (vy × vy); radial: (R − (h / 2) − M)² ≤ r2 ≤ (R + (h / 2) + M)² (each square as a product x × x); angular: σ × ((vx × S) + (vy × C)) ≥ K(φ) × √r2, with φ = ((h × a) + (2 × M)) / (2 × R) and K the cosine polynomial K(φ) = 1 − (z / 2) × (1 − (z / 12) × (1 − (z / 30) × (… × (1 − z / 552)))), z = φ × φ, the denominators being (2k − 1)(2k) for k = 1 to 12, evaluated from the innermost bracket outwards. The existence conditions give φ < π/2 + 1 < π, where K differs from cos φ by less than 10⁻¹³.
- **Off-land box.** w = h × a; box [x0, x0 + w] × [y0, y0 + h]; a unit centre is in the grown box iff x0 − M ≤ px ≤ (x0 + w) + M and y0 − M ≤ py ≤ (y0 + h) + M; the grown box is inside the id image iff x0 − M ≥ 0, (x0 + w) + M ≤ W, y0 − M ≥ 0, (y0 + h) + M ≤ H. Squared gap to a unit centre: ex = `Math.max`(x0 − px, 0, px − (x0 + w)), ey = `Math.max`(y0 − py, 0, py − (y0 + h)), g2 = (ex × ex) + (ey × ey); the box's squared gap is the smallest g2 over its set; gaps are compared as squared gaps, with Dr² = 576 and Gl² = 36. Set centre: mx = (Σpx) / n, my = (Σpy) / n; squared distance of the box centre to it: ((x0 + (w / 2)) − mx)² + ((y0 + (h / 2)) − my)² (squares as products).
- **Leader line.** From (`Math.min`(`Math.max`(px, x0), x0 + w), `Math.min`(`Math.max`(py, y0), y0 + h)) to the anchor centre (px, py).

## Functional requirements
### Shaped in-land labels
- FR-195: THE SYSTEM SHALL compute for every label the horizontal candidate exactly as 005 FR-169 (same box, same tie rule) and SHALL use it, unchanged, as the label's in-land frame whenever FR-198 does not choose a shaped frame.
- FR-196: THE SYSTEM SHALL determine, for each label set, its axis angle θ* and axis extent L as defined, and from them the shapes of the set (straight shapes at the wrapped θ* and its two wrapped neighbouring multiples of `angleStep`, except 0°; arc shapes at θ* with every bend of `arcBends` in both signs).
- FR-197: THE SYSTEM SHALL choose the shaped candidate of a label as follows: its height is hS; among the shapes with a fitting frame of height hS, the first in shape order wins; among that shape's fitting frames of height hS, the frame whose centre has the smallest squared Euclidean distance to the mean of their centres wins, then the smallest centre y, then the smallest centre x (decided exactly, "Number reference").
- FR-198: THE SYSTEM SHALL use the shaped candidate as the in-land frame IF hS is defined AND (hH is undefined OR hS ≥ G × hH); otherwise the horizontal candidate (FR-195); a horizontal candidate of height Hmax is therefore always kept.
- FR-199: THE SYSTEM SHALL decide whether a straight or arc frame exists and fits using only the unit centres, the label set, the frame, M and the formulas of "Number reference", with points on the edge of the grown footprint counted as inside, so that the decision is the same on every engine (NFR-39).
- FR-200: THE SYSTEM SHALL draw a straight-frame label with its text on its centre line in its reading direction θ, every glyph rotated by θ, upright relative to the centre line and vertically centred on it (005 vertical-centre convention), the text centred on c along the reading direction; a frame at θ = +90° reads from bottom to top (D3).
- FR-201: THE SYSTEM SHALL draw an arc-frame label with each glyph placed so that the midpoint of its advance lies on the middle line (its centre line) at its advance position of the reference glyph layout (FR-202; the text centred on c along the arc, advances measured along the middle line, total advance w), vertically centred on the middle line, and rotated to the tangent of the middle line at that point, upright relative to the arc; so the text covers radii R − h/2 to R + h/2.
- FR-202: THE SYSTEM SHALL draw every frame at every scale from the fit scale to 32 (005 FR-175), at the device pixel resolution of its displayed size (no bitmap enlarged by a factor above 1.0), with the white text and black halo of 005 FR-174, such that: (a) the **reference glyph layout** is used: the advances of the text's glyphs are measured once, at the reference font size used to measure the text aspect ratio (005), and multiplied by one factor so that their sum is w; glyph i occupies the advance interval that starts at the sum of the scaled advances before it, measured from the start of the text along the centre line (for an arc, the middle line); (b) every glyph is vertically centred on the centre line, and its on-screen reference point (the midpoint of its advance interval on the centre line) and the frame's on-screen ends and height equal those given by the frame and the reference glyph layout times the scale within 1 screen pixel or 1 % of the frame's on-screen length (along the reading direction) and height (across it), whichever is larger; (c) the halo is a stroke 2 screen pixels wide centred on the glyph outline (1 screen pixel outside it) drawn under a transform made only of translation, rotation by the frame's angle (or the arc tangent), the device pixel ratio and, for horizontal and straight frames only, a stretch along the reading direction by the factor that makes the drawn text length equal the on-screen frame length (as 005 does horizontally); so the halo is exactly 1 screen pixel wide measured across the reading direction, and is never stretched along a screen axis that is not the reading direction; arc glyphs are drawn without any stretch, so their halo is 1 screen pixel in every direction.

### Off-land labels
- FR-203: WHEN a label has no in-land frame, or its in-land height hIn is below `offLandHeight` of its role, THE SYSTEM SHALL look for an off-land box for it (FR-204, FR-205). The decision and the box are fixed per label and state, in map units, and do not depend on the scale; the box's on-screen size scales with the map like every other label (D4).
- FR-204: THE SYSTEM SHALL accept as off-land box only a horizontal box of height `offLandHeight` of the role (x0, y0 whole numbers, w = h × a) whose grown box lies inside the id image, whose grown box contains no unit centre of the label's own set, whose gap is at most Dr, and which conflicts with no obstacle of FR-215; it MAY cover sea, background, land of other territories or factions, and the label's own land outside its label set (other regions of the faction, other pieces of the territory).
- FR-205: THE SYSTEM SHALL choose, among the acceptable off-land boxes of a label, the one with the smallest gap, then the smallest squared distance from its centre to the set centre, then the smallest y0, then the smallest x0; it SHALL place off-land boxes one label at a time in ascending entry index (territory labels) or ascending faction index (faction labels), each placement seeing the ones before it.
- FR-206: IF no off-land box is acceptable for a label, THEN THE SYSTEM SHALL use its in-land frame if it has one, and otherwise draw no label for it.
- FR-207: IF an off-land box's gap is greater than Gl, THEN THE SYSTEM SHALL draw its leader line as a white (#FFFFFF) line 1 screen pixel wide over a black (#000000) halo that extends 1 screen pixel on each side of it (a black line 3 screen pixels wide under the white one, both centred on the segment, so 3 screen pixels in total; P12), at the alpha of its label (the role's opacity at the current scale times the label's opacity factor, including the crossfades of FR-210 and FR-211), only while its label is drawn (h × scale ≥ P and that alpha > 0), in a layer above the fills and every border line and below every label text of either role; leader lines MAY cross other leader lines and pass under labels of either role (the placement does not check it). An off-land box with a gap of at most Gl SHALL have no leader line.
- FR-208: THE SYSTEM SHALL draw off-land labels with the opacity, opacity factor and minimum on-screen height rules of 005 FR-172 and the ink of 005 FR-174, whatever lies under them (sea, background or land).

### Recomputation, transitions and data
- FR-209: THE SYSTEM SHALL compute every territory label's frame, off-land box and leader line from the id image, the territory and the label parameters only (once; the same at every round, also during a transition), and every faction label's frame, off-land box and leader line from the displayed state, where the obstacles of FR-215 consider only the labels of the same role in that same state.
- FR-210: WHILE a transition is playing, THE SYSTEM SHALL draw the absorber's label from its drawn frames at the earlier and later round as follows: (a) both horizontal in-land frames: one label with x0, y0 and h interpolated linearly in f exactly as 005 FR-181 (the same boxes); (b) both in-land frames, each horizontal or straight, not both horizontal: one label with its centre (for a horizontal frame (x0 + w/2, y0 + h/2)), its height h and its reading angle θ each interpolated linearly in f, θ in degrees within (−90°, +90°] without wrapping, and w = h × a; at f = 0 and at f = 1 case (b) draws the endpoint frame itself (the earlier or the later drawn frame, with its own shape and the same data as the instant label, e.g. a horizontal frame as its box), so the drawing equals the instant drawing exactly; (c) every other case (either drawn frame is an arc frame or an off-land box, or the absorber has a drawn frame at one round only, including none by FR-206): the earlier drawn frame at opacity factor 1 − f and the later one at opacity factor f, each with its own leader line, a missing side drawing nothing (the 005 fade-in or fade-out). Interpolated values SHALL equal the earlier values exactly at f = 0 and the later values exactly at f = 1.
- FR-211: WHILE a transition is playing, THE SYSTEM SHALL draw the eliminated faction's drawn frame of the earlier round (and its leader line) at opacity factor 1 − f; every territory label as at both rounds (FR-209); every other faction label whose drawn frame and leader line are the same at both rounds once, at opacity factor 1; and every other faction label whose drawn frame or leader line differs between the two rounds (because the obstacles of FR-215 changed) as the crossfade of FR-210 (c). A label, and its leader line, at opacity factor 0 SHALL draw nothing, so that the whole drawing at f = 0 and at f = 1 equals the instant drawing of the earlier and of the later round (005 FR-179).
- FR-212: THE SYSTEM SHALL give the rendering part, per label, besides the 005 FR-188 data (whose "box" is given only for a horizontal drawn frame, the 005 box, and for an off-land box; a straight or arc drawn frame has no box): its frame shape, centre, reading angle, signed curvature (1/R with the sign of β, 0 for horizontal and straight frames), height, length and, for an off-land label with a leader line, the two end points of the line; and SHALL never give it a seed, a round number, a round count or a round record.
- FR-213: THE SYSTEM SHALL compute the axis angles, extents, shapes, frames, off-land boxes, leader lines and interpolated frames with pure functions of the id image, the owners at the rounds involved, the transferred territories, the label parameters, the text aspect ratios, the scale and f, and SHALL use for a displayed round N only the rounds allowed by 005 FR-187 (0 to N, and N + 1 only while a backward step from it plays). The glyph positions along a frame (FR-201, FR-202) are not part of these functions: they are computed on the drawing side, may use trigonometric functions and are checked by the FR-202 tolerance only.
- FR-214: THE SYSTEM SHALL keep the 005 FR-190 figure covering the whole faction label layout of the last round change (including shaped and off-land placement) and SHALL show in the debug panel, separately for faction labels and territory labels of the displayed state (during a step, the state of the displayed round, that is the round being entered), the number of labels in each of these exclusive categories: horizontal, straight and arc (drawn frame is an in-land frame of that shape, FR-206 fallbacks included), off-land (an off-land box was placed), and none (no drawn frame, FR-206); only labels that have a 005 label entry at that state are counted (a faction or territory without a text aspect, a faction without land and a land entry without owner have no entry and are in no category; P13); the counts describe the layout, not the visibility at the current scale (a label hidden by P or by its opacity counts in its category). Texts through the UI strings module.
- FR-215: THE SYSTEM SHALL use as obstacles of the off-land search of a label: the in-land frames of every label of the same role in the same state, all decided before any off-land placement (whether or not those labels go off-land later, and including labels later in the placement order), and the off-land boxes of the same role already placed before it in the order of FR-205; off-land boxes of labels later in that order are not obstacles. Hence an FR-206 fallback frame never conflicts with an off-land box, and no two drawn labels of the same role conflict outside a transition.
- FR-216: THE SYSTEM SHALL give every faction label of a displayed state exactly the drawn frame, off-land box and leader line that a computation from that state alone gives (FR-209), whatever states were displayed before; a result of an earlier state MAY be reused only where it is the same by construction: an in-land frame when the label set, the text aspect ratio and the label parameters are unchanged, and an off-land box when, in addition, the whole obstacle set of its search (FR-215) is unchanged (as 005 reuses a faction box only for the same main region, aspect and parameters).

## Non-functional requirements
- NFR-38 (Performance): on the dev map, the reference machine and browser of 004 NFR-24 and a 1920 × 1080 drawing area, the 005 NFR-32 budgets hold with this spec's labels: faction labels and front distances of a round change within 100 ms (FR-190 figure), every round change drawn within 150 ms of the request, median frame time at most 16.7 ms and 95th percentile at most 33.3 ms during a transition and during panning and zooming at a scale between L0 and L1 with every allowed label drawn, and the first frame within 2 s (004 NFR-24). Territory label frames and off-land boxes are computed once per page load and count towards the first frame only. Only exact prunes are allowed (the result equals the unpruned definition), in particular: the shaped search may consider only heights h ≥ ceil(G × hH) (a lower shaped frame cannot win FR-198) and may be skipped when ceil(G × hH) > Hmax; the layout then need not know hS when it is below that bound, so hS itself is checked only through an internal function and the tests of the layout check the chosen frame. Figures recorded in the pull request.
- NFR-39 (Determinism): the functions of FR-213 (and the 005 label functions) are pure, use no DOM, graphics, clock or randomness, are tested without a browser, and give bit-identical results in Node and in every browser supported by 004 (current Chrome and Edge, the reference browsers of 004 NFR-24, and Firefox): every number follows "Number reference", so they use only operations whose results the language fixes exactly (`Math.sqrt` is allowed: the language fixes it to the correctly rounded result; on Number (double) values `**`, `Math.pow`, `Math.hypot` and trigonometric, exponential and logarithmic functions are not, so they are not used, also not in approximate prefilters such as the 005 FR-169 prefilter; BigInt multiplication and `**` in the exact part are exact and allowed), and every tie of FR-197 and FR-205 is decided by the stated order, never by iteration order of a hash or by a tolerance. The text aspect ratios stay measured outside them and passed in (005 NFR-33).
- NFR-40 (Visual verification): the review set of 005 NFR-34 is retaken and extended with fixed seed + round + camera scenes for: a steep straight label (the first with θ = +90°, else the first with θ = +75° or −75°), a diagonal straight label (any other straight angle), an arc label with β > 0 and one with β < 0, and a label that stays horizontal although a fitting shaped frame exists (hS defined and hS < G × hH); each the first hit found by a test over the territory labels in entry order, then the faction labels of seed 1 at rounds 0 to T in round order and faction order (recorded in the plan); an off-land territory label on an island, one for a small inland territory, and one with a leader line; an off-land faction label at fit scale where one exists for seed 1 (found by a test over its rounds); the crossfade at L0, between L0 and L1 and at L1 over one shaped and one off-land label (the screenshots are checked for off-land territory labels appearing mid-crossfade, edge cases); the transition of an absorber, one scene per FR-210 case: (b) a horizontal frame to a straight frame or the reverse (angle interpolation), (c) a pair involving an arc frame, and (c) a pair going between in-land and off-land, each at forward progress 0, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75 and 1 (frozen by 005 FR-191), where seed 1 to 1000 has one (first hit recorded). A scene with no hit is dropped and the drop is stated in the pull request (no hand-made map is added: map packages are out of scope). The user reviews and accepts every screenshot and the value of `offLandHeight`.
- NFR-41 (No regression): 001 to 005 tests pass with the meaning of their assertions; the dev map package, the goldens and the sim and shared exports are byte-identical; every label whose in-land frame is horizontal and that does not go off-land has exactly its 005 box. The only earlier tests that may be edited are the traceability test (expected list extended to FR-216 and NFR-42) and the 005 tests whose assertion is one of: "drawn horizontally"; pins the exact set of label draw fields; pins "every other label as at both rounds" during a transition (now FR-211); a label exists only for an entry or faction with a 005 box; an entry or faction without a 005 box has no label; or the drawn box of a label equals its 005 box (005 FR-170 and the label's drawn box change by design for off-land labels, FR-203 to FR-206, and shaped labels, FR-198). Each such edit is amended by an explicit list in the plan (test file, test title, old and new assertion). The plan SHALL also list every other earlier test that asserts the drawn box of a label rather than the horizontal candidate (for example the 005 layout tests that every faction or territory box lies inside its main region or main piece, if they check the drawn box), and state for each whether it is kept on the horizontal candidate or amended.
- NFR-42 (Dependencies and language): no dependency is added; text and leader lines are drawn with facilities the browser already provides; every new user-visible text that is not map data lives in the UI strings module (Spanish); code, comments and docs in English.

## Edge cases
- A nearly compact region (Sxx ≈ Syy, Sxy ≈ 0): θ* is arbitrary (for Sxx = Syy and a tiny Sxy > 0 it is 45°; only A = B = 0 gives 0°), and its shaped candidates usually do not reach G × hH, so the 005 box usually stays.
- A region whose horizontal box reaches Hmax: kept horizontal (FR-198), whatever its shape. More generally, with G = 1.25 and Hmax = 120 every horizontal candidate with hH ≥ 97 is kept (ceil(1.25 × 97) = 122 > 120) and the shaped search is skipped (NFR-38); at hH = 96 the only shaped height that can win is 120.
- A horizontal candidate of odd or non-multiple-of-4 height: the bound rounds up (hH = 5 gives ceil(6.25) = 7; hH = 4 gives exactly 5; hH = 1 gives 2).
- θ* = +90° (a north-south strip): straight shapes at +90°, +75° and −75° (+105° wrapped); the vertical label reads bottom to top; no arc frame exists at +90° (one end would turn past +90°).
- θ* = −75°: straight shapes at −75°, +90° (−90° wrapped) and −60°.
- θ* = ±15°: one neighbour is 0°, which is not a straight shape (the horizontal candidate covers it); two straight shapes remain.
- θ* = 0° with a bend: arc shapes only around 0° ("∪" and "∩"); straight shapes at ±15°.
- An arc whose ends would turn past ±90°, or whose inner radius R − h/2 − M is not positive, or whose set has L = 0 (one unit): that frame does not exist; a smaller h may still exist (except for L = 0).
- Equal shaped heights in two shapes: shape order decides (FR-197), never the order in which they were tried.
- Two labels with the same text: placed independently (005); off-land boxes still may not conflict.
- An island whose only acceptable off-land box is far (gap > Dr): falls back to its tiny in-land frame, drawn only at scales where it reaches P (005 FR-172), or no label (FR-206).
- A small inland territory surrounded by neighbours with large labels: its off-land box may cover neighbouring land but not their labels; it may end up with a leader line.
- An off-land search blocked by the tiny in-land frame of another label that itself goes off-land: accepted (FR-215 counts every in-land frame), the price of a fallback never conflicting.
- A territory whose main piece is an island and whose other pieces are on the mainland: label and off-land search use the main piece only (005 FR-168); the off-land box may lie on its other pieces (FR-204).
- A faction reduced to an island or a sliver: its faction label goes off-land (FR-203), avoiding the other factions' labels of that state.
- A faction with hIn = 17: it goes off-land and is drawn at height 18, outside its land, for one unit of height gained (accepted, D4 trigger).
- Off-land territory labels (height 5) reach P = 9 screen pixels only from scale 1.8, inside the L0 to L1 crossfade: they appear there at territory opacity smoothstep(1.5, 2, 1.8) ≈ 0.65 rather than fading in from 0 (accepted; looked for in the NFR-40 crossfade screenshots).
- Off-land territory labels near the image edge: a box whose grown box leaves the image is not acceptable (as 005 plan D12).
- Faction label and territory labels crossfading over the same place: allowed to overlap as in 005 (different roles never conflict); leader lines may cross labels and other leader lines (FR-207).
- During a transition the absorber's moving label may overlap other labels (accepted, as in 005); an absorber whose drawn frame is an arc or off-land at either round crossfades instead of moving.
- A third faction whose off-land box moves or falls back because the absorber's frame changed or the eliminated label disappeared: it crossfades between its two drawn frames (FR-211).
- An absorber whose earlier frame is a straight frame at +90° and later at −75°: the angle passes through 0° (linear, no wrap) so the text never reads upside down.
- Leader line of an off-land label whose box is nearly touching: no line while the gap ≤ Gl.
- Round T (one faction) on the dev map: its label is in-land (the whole land is large enough); no off-land faction label. On another map a single remaining faction could still go off-land.
- Reduced-motion preference and frozen `transition` (005 FR-177, FR-191): unchanged; frozen frames show the interpolated frames and crossfades of FR-210 and FR-211.

## Risks
- **Layout cost.** Straight and arc frames multiply the candidates by up to 3 + 2 × |`arcBends`| = 7 shapes, and off-land search scans around each label set. Faction labels must still fit 100 ms per change (005 needed an exact speed-up to pass). Mitigations allowed by this spec: the reuse of FR-216 (narrower than 005 plan D5 for off-land boxes), the height prune of NFR-38, skipping shaped search when ceil(G × hH) > Hmax, and other exact (not approximate) pruning. If the budget fails, the fix is a spec change (fewer shapes, smaller Dr), not an approximation.
- **Monotonicity of the height search.** For a fixed shape and centre a smaller h gives a footprint inside the larger one only because R = L / |β| does not depend on h; changing that definition would break exact maximal-height search.
- **Float ties.** Rotated and arc footprints compare unit centres with non-integer limits. "Number reference" fixes the formulas and their evaluation order, so the answer is the same on every engine even where it differs from exact geometry by a rounding; the brute-force tests use the same formulas.
- **Arc text rendering.** Glyph-by-glyph placement on an arc may kern differently from straight text and the halo may overlap between glyphs; the FR-202 tolerance and the screenshots decide.
- **Arc margin at the ends.** The grown arc footprint gives the full margin M beyond the text ends only along the middle line; at the inner edge it is smaller and the rotated end glyphs may poke slightly out of the sector (Definitions, P11). An arc label may therefore come slightly closer than M to its set's edge near its inner end corners; the screenshots decide.
- **Placement order effects.** Off-land boxes placed in index order can push a later label away (leader line) or out (FR-206) where another order would fit both; the in-land frames of labels that go off-land also block (FR-215). Accepted for determinism, visible in the review set.
- **Shape flicker across rounds.** A faction whose region changes a little may switch between horizontal and shaped; the G rule limits it; the transition interpolates horizontal and straight changes and crossfades the others.
- **Halo under rotation.** The 005 drawing stretches the whole drawing (stroke included) horizontally to correct the width; under rotation that stretch must be applied along the reading direction, never along the screen x axis, and arcs are not stretched (FR-202 (c)).

## Decisions
Answered by the user on 2026-10-18 (each the option recommended in the draft).
- D1. Curves: **one circular arc** along the region's axis, bend from a small set (`arcBends` {30°, 60°}). Why: simple to specify and test, and the height search stays exact and monotone. Discarded: a free curve along the region's skeleton (hard to specify, risk of another patch spiral); no curves. Covers FR-196, FR-197, FR-201.
- D2. A shaped label replaces the horizontal one **only when it is at least G times taller** (G = 1.5 as answered on 2026-10-18; lowered to 1.25 by D9); tried angles are the axis angle θ* and its two 15° neighbours, plus the arcs. Why: most 005 labels stay unchanged, cost is bounded, less flicker between rounds. Discarded: every multiple of 15° (about 4 times the cost); always the tallest frame (G = 1). Covers FR-196, FR-198, `shapeGain`.
- D3. Vertical text reads **from bottom to top**. Why: the usual map convention. Discarded: top to bottom. Covers the reading-direction definition and FR-200.
- D4. Off-land is a **fixed decision per label**: off-land when the in-land height is below `offLandHeight` (territory 5, faction 18 map units, about 10 screen pixels at L1 and at fit scale), drawn at that height, scaling with the map. Why: no layout work while zooming, deterministic. Discarded: a scale-dependent decision with constant on-screen size. Covers FR-203, FR-204, `offLandHeight`.
- D5. Off-land boxes go to the **nearest free spot anywhere** (sea, background or other land) that hides no label of the same role, within Dr = 24 map units, with a **1 px white leader line with black halo** when the gap exceeds Gl = 6 map units. Why: islands and small inland territories both get a readable, attached name. Discarded: sea and background only; sea first then land; black or no leader lines. Covers FR-204, FR-205, FR-207, `offLandReach`, `leaderGap`.
- D6. The debug panel **shows the label counts** (horizontal, straight, arc, off-land, none). Why: makes the review set checkable at a glance. Discarded: timing only. Covers FR-214.

Answered by the user on 2026-10-19 (QA review).
- D7. `arcBends` is **fixed** at {30°, 60°}; the only tunable parameter is `offLandHeight` (G, Dr, Gl, `angleStep` fixed). Why: fewer moving parts in the review; the pinned hash stays meaningful. Discarded: tunable bends.
- D8. The bend β is **the turn over the region's axis extent L** (R = L / |β|), not over the label length. Why: R does not depend on h, which keeps the height search exact and monotone. Discarded: the turn over the label length w (R would change with h). Covers the arc frame definition.

Answered by the user on 2026-10-19 (review of the T8 screenshots; amendment made after T1 to T8 were implemented).
- D9. `shapeGain` G is **1.25** (was 1.5), still fixed, not tunable. Why: on the T8 screenshots several territories whose shape is clearly diagonal (Castilla-La Mancha, Trentino, Friuli) kept a small horizontal name because their shaped frame was taller than the horizontal one by less than 1.5 times; 1.25 lets a clearly taller diagonal win while a near-compact region still keeps its 005 box. Discarded: 1.1 (shape changes for marginal gains, more flicker of faction labels between rounds, more labels differing from 005); keeping 1.5 (the diagonals the user pointed out stay horizontal). Consequences: the height prune of NFR-38 becomes ceil(1.25 × hH), the shaped search is skipped for hH ≥ 97, and the pinned parameter value, the layout digest, the dev-map counts and the NFR-40 hits taken with 1.5 are re-taken. Covers FR-198, NFR-38, NFR-40, `shapeGain`.
- Not a spec change (same review): Bremen and Hamburg are handled by tuning `offLandHeight` of territories inside its range 3 to 12 during the NFR-40 review (candidates 3 and 4); `leaderGap` stays 6 (a box at gap ≤ 6 has no leader line, FR-207).

Planner decisions on 2026-10-19 (QA review; the simplest deterministic option, for the user to see in the diff).
- P1. (FR-211, FR-216) Another faction's label that differs between the two rounds of a step **crossfades** (FR-211); a label at opacity factor 0 draws nothing; reuse of earlier results only where exact (FR-216). Why: f = 0 and f = 1 equal the instant drawings with no new motion rule. Discarded: moving such labels (a second interpolation rule); keeping the earlier box during the step (breaks f = 1).
- P2. (FR-204, FR-215; confirmed by the user on 2026-10-19: a label that goes off-land keeps blocking with its in-land frame) Obstacles are the in-land frames of **every** same-role label plus the off-land boxes placed earlier (FR-215); an off-land box's **grown** box must contain no unit of its own set; the label's own non-main land may be covered (FR-204). Why: a fallback can never conflict, so "no two drawn labels of the same role conflict" holds without exceptions. Discarded: only the in-land frames of labels that stay in-land (more room, but a fallback could then conflict with a box placed before it).
- P3. (FR-197, FR-199, NFR-39) **Exact integers for θ* and the FR-197 tie, pinned double formulas for everything else**, with a cosine polynomial (only + − × ÷) for the arc's angular limit ("Number reference"). Why: θ* depends only on integer sums, while rotated and arc tests need irrational constants that exact arithmetic would turn into nested radicals; pinned doubles are bit-identical on every engine because the language fixes these operations. Discarded: exact algebraic evaluation of every fit test (complex, slow); unpinned float with trigonometric functions (engine-dependent).
- P4. (FR-196) Neighbouring angles **wrap** into (−90°, +90°]. Why: symmetric for strips near vertical. Discarded: dropping angles outside the range (asymmetric).
- P5. (FR-210; confirmed by the user on 2026-10-19) Absorber pairs with an arc frame **crossfade** instead of interpolating curvature (FR-210 (c)). Why: horizontal and straight frames stay valid at every f (their angle range is convex), while interpolated curvature and height could break R − h/2 − M > 0 or turn an end past ±90° mid-step. Discarded: interpolating curvature with a validity check at every f.
- P6. (FR-207) Leader lines are drawn **only while their label is drawn**, at its alpha; crossings and passing under labels are accepted. Discarded: routing lines around labels (out of scope).
- P7. (FR-202, FR-210) Reference glyph layout and halo transform of FR-202; the horizontal pair of FR-210 interpolates x0, y0, h exactly as 005. Why: testable, and horizontal labels keep their 005 look bit for bit. Discarded: removing the 005 stretch for all labels (would change approved 005 drawings).
- P8. (NFR-38) Height prune h ≥ ceil(G × hH) allowed, and the shaped search skipped when ceil(G × hH) > Hmax; tests check the chosen frame. Discarded: requiring hS for every label (cost with no visible effect).
- P9. (NFR-40) A review scene without a hit on the dev map is **dropped** and reported in the pull request; "steep" means +90° first, else ±75°. Discarded: a hand-made review map (a new map package, out of scope).
- P10. (FR-214) Debug counts are **exclusive layout categories** of the displayed state, fallbacks counted by shape, visibility ignored. Discarded: counting only visible labels (changes while zooming, harder to compare between screenshots).
- P11. (Definitions, FR-199) The arc's grown footprint keeps the angular half-width φ = (w + 2M) / (2R), with the margin **approximate at the ends** (full M along the middle line, less at the inner edge; end glyphs may poke out slightly). Why: the exact-looking alternative φ = (w/2 + M) / (R − h/2 − M) grows without bound as the inner radius tends to 0, so φ < π and the validity of the K(φ) cosine polynomial (error below 10⁻¹³) would no longer hold, and it is still not exact (r × sin(φ − φ0) < M). Discarded: that alternative; an exact Minkowski sum (offset lines, not radial lines: harder to specify and test).
- P12. (FR-207) The leader line halo is 1 screen pixel on each side of a 1 pixel white line (3 pixels in total), drawn as a 3 px black line under a 1 px white line. Why: a line has no outline to centre a stroke on; this gives the same 1 pixel of black on each side as the text halo. Discarded: a 2 px stroke centred on the line outline (undefined for a 1 px line; would read thinner).
- P13. (FR-214) The debug counts cover only labels that have a 005 label entry at the displayed state; factions or territories without a text aspect, factions without land and unowned entries are not counted. Why: "none" then means exactly "eligible but no drawn frame" (FR-206), which is what the review needs. Discarded: counting them as "none" (mixes data gaps with layout failures).
